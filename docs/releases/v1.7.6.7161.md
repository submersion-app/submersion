# 🎉 Submersion v1.7.6.7161 Release Notes

v1.7.6 brings 58 merged changes since v1.7.5. The headliners are dive computers that finally join your gear on every dive they logged, a rebuilt 3D dive view with a metric of your choice on the third axis, historical weather backfill for the whole logbook, SAC and RMV separated into the two quantities they always were, and a set of sync and restore fixes that close every known path to a logbook suddenly showing no dives. This release upgrades the database (schema 160 to 175), so please read the upgrade notes at the end.

---

## ✨ New and improved

### Your dive computer joins your gear

Submersion has always known which computer downloaded each dive, but the device never appeared in the dive's Equipment section, earned no service tracking, and could not be filtered on. Now every registered dive computer gets a matching gear item, each downloaded dive is linked to it automatically, and a one-time migration backfills your existing logbook, including dives registered by past file imports. Service intervals, purchase details, gear statistics, and equipment filters all work for the device you actually take on every dive. The computer appears on a dive both in the Details card, which opens the device page with firmware and download history, and as a gear tile, which opens the equipment page with service and purchase records; the two destinations are different on purpose.

### The 3D dive view becomes an instrument

The single-dive 3D view was a depth ribbon that showed nothing the 2D chart does not. It is rebuilt as a genuine three-axis chart: run time on X, depth on Y, and a metric you pick on Z, from temperature, ascent rate, tank pressure, ppO2, CNS, heart rate, and time to surface. The path casts shadows on the three walls of the scene, and each shadow is itself a readable 2D chart (depth against time, metric against time, depth against metric), with camera presets that snap each wall front-on. Axes carry unit-aware ticks and labels, hovering shows a tooltip anchored to the path, and the readout panel and marker sheet share one set of figures.

### Weather for the dives that never had any

Until now only a newly saved dive fetched weather. A new Fetch Conditions for All Dives action in the dive list menu backfills historical conditions across your whole logbook. The fill is per field and never overwrites anything: a dive with a wind speed but no cloud cover keeps its wind speed and gains a cloud cover, hand-entered weather is never relabelled as provider data, and your own written description is never touched. Requests are grouped by place and day, so a liveaboard week costs one archive call per day rather than one per dive, and the run shows progress and can be cancelled. Trip day weather is also now fetched once, stored with the trip, and synced, and it covers travel, port, and sea days instead of only surface days.

### SAC and RMV separated at last

SAC (pressure drop per minute, a property of one cylinder) and RMV (surface volume per minute, a property of the diver) were one value with a unit toggle, which guaranteed the label was wrong in one of the two modes. They are now separate quantities. Settings > Units > Gas consumption offers SAC, RMV, or Both; existing users land on the lane they were already seeing. The dive detail shows a row per lane, the cylinders card shows one line per lane per cylinder, the consumption-by-segment and gas statistics pages get a SAC / RMV switch, and the dive table gets independent, sortable SAC and RMV columns, with saved layouts migrated. German keeps AMV for the volume rate and uses Druckverbrauch for the pressure rate. Alongside this, dive-computer downloads finally honour the apply-default-tank setting, so a downloaded dive without a recorded cylinder volume can compute a volume rate instead of silently hiding the row.

### Pin a photo to its moment in the dive

A camera with a wrongly set clock used to produce a confident marker at the dive's entry or exit and a nonsense elapsed-time chip, with no way to correct it. The elapsed chip in the media viewer is now tappable and opens a dialog with a time field, a slider across the dive, and a live mini-profile preview of where the shot will sit; Reset returns to automatic. A capture time far outside the dive is now shown as unknown instead of being clamped to the entry or exit, and when linking media to dives, capture-time mismatches are explained and can be corrected on the spot.

### Sites that fill themselves in

Picking coordinates used to fill only country and region. Every coordinate source now also fills the town and the body of water, a Look up from coordinates action on the site form fills empty fields (and offers Replace or Keep when a lookup disagrees with what is there), and a Fill in missing location details sweep in the sites list walks every site that has coordinates and completes only the empty fields, with progress and cancel. A new synced Place name language setting controls the language the lookups answer in. Body of water works for lakes, reservoirs, rivers, bays, and straits; the open sea has no named polygons, and islands stay a manual field because the data source does not carry them.

### Richer site and buddy cards

Site list cards gain a rating, difficulty, water type, one chip per site feature (wreck, mooring, swim-through, and friends), a depth range, dive count, last dived date, and your own max depth there, all configurable through the card layout settings that existed but were never wired up. Buddy cards gain an avatar ring in the certification agency's colour, a certification chip, dives together, the last shared dive, the buddy's usual role, and contact icons. Both lists get true compact variants.

### Sharper search and a smarter buddy picker

Advanced Search gains a Decompression filter (Any, Yes, or No, derived from the recorded profile the same way the deco statistic is) and a weekday filter that combines with a date range, so "Saturdays last summer" is now a query. The Add Buddy picker can sort buddies by how many dives you have shared, and buddies can be starred as favorites that pin to the top regardless of sort. All contributed by @alpheios-one.

### A better GPS log page

On desktop the GPS Log was mostly empty space. The track list now sits beside the overview map: tapping a track selects it, frames it, and floats a details card. Every width gets a summary strip with track count, recorded time, and dives covered, plus a real empty state instead of a lone sentence.

### Smaller additions

- **Dive type badges** (Wreck, Night, Drift, and the rest) appear in the dive detail header and on the list cards, collapsing to a "+N" badge when space runs out. Custom types can set their own short name, and every type gets visibility toggles for the header and the lists. Contributed by @readme42.
- **The divider between the list and detail panes is now draggable.** Contributed by @readme42.
- Whether the **CCR O2 cell curves** are shown by default is now a setting. Contributed by @readme42.
- **Subsurface imports now carry over the photos** attached to each dive.
- **Safety review findings can be dismissed in bulk**, on one dive or across the whole logbook, always scoped to the rules you have switched on.
- The bundled **recompression chamber directory** grew from 14 chambers to 209 across 59 countries, each entry verified against a named source.
- **Sideloaded Android installs now check for updates** and show the update banner. Submersion is not on Google Play quite yet (but almost!), so previously they never learned a new build existed.
- Debug log exports name the exact build they came from.
- Layout polish: Media Library selection follows the shared selection pattern, wide dive-detail panes pair Surface GPS with Tide and Cylinders with Weights, and the Site Features section moved below the depth statistics.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

**Sync, backup, and restore**

Four fixes close every known path to a logbook that suddenly shows no dives:

- **A restore could be undone by the next sync.** With no recorded sync time, which is exactly the state after a recovery, deletions recorded by other devices were applied without any age check. Deletions are now compared against the record's own edit history, and a disagreement surfaces as a conflict instead of a silent delete.
- **Restoring from a backup whose file had gone missing reported "Restore Complete"** while restoring nothing at all. It now reports the missing file as the error it is.
- **A stale active-diver reference showed an empty logbook** even though every dive was still in the database. The app now detects and repairs the reference on its own.
- **Adopting an existing sync collection, or rejoining after retiring a device, ignored the schema compatibility floor.** Both now hold until the out-of-date device updates, the same way ordinary sync does.

And the rest:

- Publishing sync base data from a Windows device wrote into a phantom "Temp" subfolder because paths were joined with mixed separators, breaking sync setup from Windows.
- iCloud backups were uploaded into the sync data folder. They now land in Submersion Backups.
- A hung Dropbox or Google Drive request could stall sync indefinitely. Cloud requests now carry deadlines.
- The Resolve Conflicts dialog dumped raw database columns for entities without a name, such as a dive's tags. It now describes them in words.

**Dive computers and imports**

- On a multi-tank dive with several transmitters, only one pressure reading per sample was kept, so the other transmitters' data was lost. libdivecomputer reports one pressure per transmitter, and every one of them now comes through.
- Downloading from a saved computer connected straight to the stored address without scanning first, which could fail to find the device. A scan now precedes the connection.
- Dives imported from files registered no dive computer, so they could not be attributed to or filtered by the computer that logged them.
- Cylinder end pressure was read at the very end of the recording rather than at surfacing. On a rebreather whose closed oxygen valve bleeds the hose down after the dive, that overstated oxygen consumption roughly tenfold.
- Gauge-mode dives grew an estimated tank pressure line for a gas they were not breathing, with no way to remove it. Gauge dives no longer estimate pressure, and estimates can be switched off entirely.
- An OSTC Sport was labelled as the wrong model.

**Media**

- A media transfer queue that stopped, for any reason, never restarted; uploads could sit pending forever, especially on desktop where the app runs in one process for days. The queue now resumes itself.
- Adding an iOS Live Photo failed with a bare framework error code. It is now reported as unsupported, which it currently is.

**Statistics and lists**

- Personal records in Statistics ignored the active filter.
- The Deco and No Deco legend labels were attached to the wrong bar segments.
- A certification entered with a custom name showed only its agency on the card, hiding the certification itself.
- A buddy's shared-dives list was not sorted by dive date and omitted the year.
- The detailed dive site list showed depths in meters regardless of your unit settings, and the depth filter compared imperial input directly against metric values, so an imperial diver filtering at 100 was actually filtering at 100 meters.

**Interface and platforms**

- On iPad, exporting UDDF blanked the screen, and export share sheets opened detached from the control that opened them. Garmin FIT imports also now explain why tank pressure is absent instead of showing nothing.
- In the 3D site view, two-finger zoom now works, the zoom buttons are no longer covered, and the appearance sheet can actually be closed.
- On Windows, app data has moved out of a legacy company-named directory; the move happens automatically on first launch.

## ⚠️ Upgrade notes

- **The database upgrades from schema 160 to 175.** The migration runs once on first launch, and the app takes an automatic backup beforehand, so there is nothing you need to do first. Update your other devices reasonably soon: one migration renames a synced column, which raises the sync compatibility floor, so devices on older builds will be held from syncing until they update.
- **Every registered dive computer gains a gear item,** and its downloaded dives are linked to it, retroactively. If you had already added your computer as equipment by hand, that item is adopted rather than duplicated. Expect Most Used Gear to be topped by your dive computer, since it is on every downloaded dive.
- **A small buoyancy correction:** dive computers previously fell into a 0.5 kg default dry mass in the buoyancy planner. They now count 0.0 kg unless you set a weight yourself, so a rig that included a hand-added computer shifts slightly.
- **Your gas consumption display carries over.** The old SAC unit toggle maps to the matching lane: L/min becomes RMV, pressure per minute becomes SAC. The numbers themselves are computed exactly as before, and new installs default to showing both.
- **No minimum operating system change** on any platform.
