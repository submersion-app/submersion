# 🎉 Submersion v1.7.3.6027 Release Notes

v1.7.3 is the largest release so far: 176 merged changes since v1.7.2. The headliners are optional password protection and at-rest encryption for your dive log, recorded GPS surface tracks that are finally drawn on a map, media attachments on dive sites, and a home screen you can arrange yourself. Alongside those is a long run of fixes to dive computer downloads, imports, sync, and the profile chart. This release upgrades the database (schema 137 to 150), so please read the upgrade notes at the end.

---

## ✨ New and improved

### App security: lock the app, and encrypt the database

Two independent protections now sit under Settings > App Security, sharing one password and one 8-word recovery code. **App Lock** gates the app at launch with your password or biometrics and re-locks after a configurable time in the background. **Database Encryption** encrypts `submersion.db` itself with SQLCipher, so the file is unreadable if someone copies it off your device. Either can be used without the other: App Lock alone works fine against a normal database.

Encryption is opt-in, converts your existing database in place, and takes a safety backup first. Changing your password rewrites one small key file rather than re-encrypting the whole database. Backup files stay in the portable plaintext format so they remain restorable anywhere; use an encrypted backup if you want the archive protected too. If you lose the password, the recovery code is the only way back in.

**Encryption can affect performance.** Once it is on, every read and write goes through SQLCipher, so large dive logs may feel slower to open, scroll, and search. The settings entry and the confirmation dialog both say so before you enable it. App Lock on its own has no such cost, so if what you want is a lock at launch rather than protection of the file itself, use App Lock alone.

### Your recorded GPS tracks are now on the map

Submersion has recorded GPS surface tracks for several releases but only ever listed them as rows with a fix count. Tracks are now drawn: a thumbnail on each row, a full detail page with the track colored by speed or time, an overview map of every track you have recorded, and a Surface GPS section on the dive detail page for tracks that line up with a dive. You can import tracks from GPX, KML, CSV, and FIT files, export to GPX or KML, and trim or split a track without destroying the original. Coordinates can be entered and displayed in decimal degrees, degrees-decimal-minutes, degrees-minutes-seconds, UTM, or MGRS.

Dive computers that report a position per sample, such as Ratio models, now have those fixes captured from the profile as well.

### Photos, videos, and documents on dive sites

Dive sites can hold their own media: entry-point photos, video, hand-drawn underwater maps, and documents. PDFs render inside the app and show their first page as the tile; other formats open in your system viewer. The site detail page gains a Site Media section that shows the site's own attachments plus a collapsed group of photos from dives logged there. Documents can be attached to dives as well.

Site media is **linked, not copied**, exactly like dive media. Submersion stores a reference to the file where it already lives, in your photo library or on disk, and never puts the photo, video, or document itself into the dive log database. Moving, renaming, or deleting the original breaks the link, and a backup of your dive log carries the references rather than the files. If you want the actual files carried between devices, configure a media store under Settings.

### A customizable home screen

Settings > Appearance > Home now lets you hide, show, and reorder the dashboard cards. The default order reproduces the previous layout exactly, so nothing moves until you move it. Urgent banners stay pinned at the top and cannot be hidden. Every gauge chip in the home header is now tappable and opens the page that explains the number behind it, and the sync chip reflects whether you have unsynced changes rather than only the last sync result.

### Safety findings you can see on the profile

Post-dive safety findings are now anchored to the dive profile chart. Selecting a finding highlights the exact stretch of the dive it refers to, in a dedicated lane with a callout, and very short events are given a minimum visible width so they are still clickable. The review also runs automatically after a backup restore, so a freshly restored log is assessed rather than left unchecked.

### Multi-select works the same way everywhere

Selection has been standardized across the app: dives, sites, buddies, tags, media, equipment, and more, all behave identically. The checkbox now sits inside the item card, shift-click and modifier-click anchor on the highlighted row, and long-press no longer drops you into selection mode by accident. Bulk delete moved behind the overflow menu so it is harder to hit unintentionally.

### Reef and water conditions

Dive sites can pull reef data from four online sources, and the old "reef health" section has been reframed as water conditions so it makes sense at sites that are not reefs. Conditions auto-fill moved to the top of the form and derives altitude from the site's GPS position.

### Import dives from a Garmin over USB

Garmin devices connected by USB can now be imported directly, without going through Garmin Connect.

### Smaller additions

- **Closed-circuit rebreathers are a first-class equipment type**, with cylinder configurations and rebreather-specific service tracking.
- **Save a tank as a reusable preset** from the dive edit form.
- **Currency presets and a default currency** for equipment values, with correct symbols.
- **A first-sync date cutoff** for dive computer downloads, so a new computer does not drag in years of dives you have already logged.
- **Optional per-feature color accents** for the interface.
- **Certification card photos** appear in the wallet, the Level dropdown is now called Certification, and buddy professional roles fold into their certifications.
- **Trip pages show the weather** for each day, including surface days, and the remaining dive window before a return flight.
- **The visibility scale is calibrated**: you record the measured distance and the adjective is derived from it.
- **Checklists** gained a filter and Excel export, and unreadable titles were fixed.
- **Exports offer Save to file** everywhere they previously only offered Share.
- **The dive planner honours your gradient factor settings**, keeps an open plan when settings change, and lets you set the gas mix for the second dive in the surface interval tool.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

**Startup and platform**

- Submersion crashed on launch on macOS 11 Big Sur before the window appeared. The app bundle's symbol table was being written in an order the older loader could not search.
- The iOS deployment target is now 15.0. iOS 14 devices are no longer supported.
- Display zoom below 100% stopped taps registering near the screen edges.
- The Android system back button closed the app instead of going back a page.
- Settings sub-sections slide into view and return to Settings with the system back gesture instead of exiting.
- Linux no longer silently resets or ignores a custom database folder, and Android stops offering cloud-synced folders it cannot actually use for the database.

**Dive computers**

- Shearwater downloads that aborted with zero dives now retry and keep partial transfers, and deleting a dive computer works again.
- Heinrichs Weikamp computers use the Terminal I/O credit handshake over BLE, fixing stalled downloads.
- OSTC models with a suffixed Bluetooth name resolve to the right descriptor instead of failing to connect.
- macOS USB-serial downloads were being denied by the App Sandbox.
- USB is no longer advertised for computers that are USB-HID only.
- A stale Apple pairing is recognised and cleared, and a computer with zero dives can still be saved.
- Android downloads continue when Bluetooth pairing does not complete, and proactive bonding is skipped for Shearwater devices that do not want it.
- Every skipped Bluetooth scan result is logged, so an unsupported computer shows up rather than silently never appearing.
- Divesoft Liberty profiles no longer log phantom 0 m samples.

**Sync, backup, and restore**

- A backend that had been wiped could never be recovered from; it now converges, and duplicate tags are no longer created in the process.
- Restoring a large backup could freeze the interface or fail intermittently on close.
- Sync stopped re-running a stale cold-start restore on every cycle, which made every sync slow.
- S3 sync was unreliable on mobile networks.
- Replaying changes from a device on a different version now fills in columns it did not know about, instead of writing nulls.
- Scheduled backups honour the cloud switch instead of always writing locally.
- Reset and rebuild now say honestly what they do, offer a replace-from-this-device action, and name your sync peers.
- The setup wizard no longer claims "No library found" for an encrypted or partly published cloud library.

**Import and export**

- MacDive imports decode the SQLite dive profiles and no longer lose data.
- UDDF imports handle float-formatted integers, after-dive lead weight, tank volumes in the wrong unit, the spec ppO2 elements, and circuit names.
- Shearwater Cloud imports carry per-cell O2 readings through.
- CSV imports parse LF and byte-order-mark files correctly, and there is a MySSI preset.
- Importing a site that duplicates an existing one now offers to overwrite it.
- Skipped duplicate buddies and tags link to the existing record instead of being dropped.
- PDF export honours the detail level you picked and prints total runtime.
- Sequential merges from the same computer no longer leave duplicate data sources on the dive.

**Dives, profiles, and statistics**

- Bottom time was wrong for multilevel profiles.
- Safety stops were over-detected on wavy shallow phases.
- An unreachable surface interval was reported as six hours.
- The post-dive review reported ascents that never happened.
- Lifetime totals now count your prior dives.
- Statistics ascent and descent rates are derived from depth samples instead of coming out empty.
- Segment SAC falls back proportionally to dive duration and uses the per-segment tank volume.
- Statistics chart grid lines line up with their y-axis labels.
- Secondary-axis metrics scale to the visible depth window, and the chart no longer animates while you interact with it.
- Photo markers on the profile respond to a tap immediately.
- On Android the profile chart pans when zoomed and scrubs instantly.
- The profile fills the whole screen in fullscreen on phones, with the readout kept clear of the close button.
- The profile legend fills its row on one line.
- Water temperature tooltips are formatted in your units.
- The NDL overlay continues as TTS once you are in deco.
- Buddy filtering in the dive list matches correctly, buddy roles survive bulk edits, and a picked role in bulk edit is saved.
- Opening a dive from a list keeps it on the navigation stack instead of redirecting to the master-detail view.
- Course pages and trip pages stop showing a dive after it has been merged away.

**Sites, trips, and equipment**

- Tide predictions were inaccurate; the harmonic engine had errors and station selection has been refined. Tide times no longer shift to device-local time.
- Reverse geocoding is pinned to English so site grouping is consistent.
- The site picker sorts by distance after a dive computer import.
- 3D History is reachable from the master-detail site pane.
- Retired gear is hidden from pickers and the default equipment list.
- Equipment-set caches no longer go stale after deleting gear, and type-specific fields show when adding gear on a narrow window.
- Maintenance-due taps open the equipment item rather than the list.
- Shared trips find matching dives again, and the trip end-date picker opens at the start date you just picked.
- Course dates more than a year out are allowed.

**Interface and media**

- Exiting a photo opened from the home photo ribbon crashed on iPhone.
- The home photo ribbon refreshes when a photo is deleted, and tapping a photo opens the viewer.
- Attaching a document on Android uses the storage URI rather than a cache path that had already gone.
- Sharing a photo or video could crash on a blank filename or a missing temporary directory.
- Data quality cleanup repairs now actually clear the findings they fixed.
- The species sighting count and the entity table column picker update live.
- Fractional cylinder sizes stay intact in tank displays.
- The date format setting is honoured in manual date entry and in the remaining date displays.
- Stacked text fields no longer overlap the field above, and segmented-button labels stop wrapping mid-word on phones.
- Diver-profile save actions are visible on same-color app bars.
- The Add New Buddy screen appears in front of the bulk-edit dialog.
- "Open Full Page" works on desktop, and the dive number badge no longer wraps.
- Release notes are readable in dark-mode update dialogs.
- Report an Issue launches reliably and offers a copyable fallback link.
- The remaining Transfer and dive-computer strings are translated.
- Windows auto-updates are verified with a signature before installing.

## ⚠️ Upgrade notes

- **The database is upgraded** from schema 137 to 150 on first launch. A backup is taken automatically. Update every device that syncs together, ideally before syncing.
- **iOS 14 is no longer supported.** The minimum is now iOS 15.0.
- **Photos linked before this release keep their shifted capture time** in the fullscreen viewer. The stored value was off by your time zone offset. Existing entries are not rewritten, because doing so would corrupt the ones that were always correct; unlink and re-add a photo to fix its displayed time. Matching against your photo library keeps working for the old entries either way.
- **Database encryption may affect performance.** Every read and write goes through SQLCipher, which is most noticeable on large dive logs and on older phones. It is opt-in for that reason, and the app warns you both in Settings and in the confirmation dialog before turning it on. App Lock without encryption has no performance cost.
- **Keep your recovery code.** If you enable App Lock or encryption and forget the password, the 8-word recovery code is the only way back into your dive log.
