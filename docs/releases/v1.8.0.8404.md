# 🎉 Submersion v1.8.0.8404 Release Notes

v1.8.0 brings 138 merged changes since v1.7.10. The headline is photo and video sync, rebuilt end to end: a photo linked on one device now finds the same photo on every other device, a device can no longer mark a photo missing that it never had, the transfer queue always says what it is waiting for, deletes propagate, and a media health report shows exactly where a photo stands on each device. The sync engine fix underneath it also stops dives, buddies and sites diverging when two devices edit the same record apart. Beyond that: logging a dive into a buddy's profile and filling planned dives from a dive computer download, a Diving Log and DiveLogDT logbook importer, tags on equipment, each CCR O2 cell drawn as its own curve on a much cleaner profile chart, gear service status shown wherever gear appears, a fixed header and map on Site Details, 3D terrain that sharpens as you zoom, real ways back in when the dive log cannot be read, and restored accents across seven translations. This release upgrades the database (schema 218 to 226) and raises the sync compatibility floor, so please read the upgrade notes at the end.

---

## ✨ New and improved

### Photos and videos that follow you between devices

Media sync has been the most persistent source of bug reports: placeholders on the second device, photos marked missing by a device that never had them, transfers stalled with no explanation, deletes that never arrived. Three systems are involved (the record in sync, the other device finding the bytes, and the transfer queue feeding your media store), and every fix to one of them left the handoffs untouched. This release ships the program that fixes them as one, tested on a two-device harness.

**The same photo on every device.** Any device, on any platform, looks for a linked photo in its own photo library by filename, capture time and dimensions, and in your media store (iCloud Drive, S3, Google Drive or Dropbox) as soon as the store holds it, even before the upload record has synced. On Apple devices sharing an iCloud Photos library, the photo's iCloud identifier is matched first, so bursts and near-identical frames resolve to the exact photo.

**Only the device that can know marks a photo missing.** A gallery miss counts as missing only on the device that linked the photo. On that device, a limited photo selection, a failed gallery query or a lost Android read grant now writes nothing, and under limited access a tile reads "Not in your allowed photos" with "Allow full access" and "Choose photo again" actions.

**The sync engine no longer drops an edit.** When two devices edited the same record apart, the newer edit never landed and the two diverged permanently. That affected dives, buddies and sites too. The newer edit now wins on both devices, upload stamps can no longer make an older caption win, and this is the change that raises the sync compatibility floor.

**Transfers never wait silently.** The Media Storage summary now says why the queue is stopped ("Waiting for a connection", a detached or unreachable store, or a marker mismatch with a "Reconnect media storage" action), and a failed transfer reports what Dropbox, S3, Google Drive or the file system actually said. "Connect Google Drive" opens the sign-in instead of failing.

**Deletes propagate.** Deleting a diver now removes their photos and documents on every device, including uploaded copies in the store, the way deleting a single dive or site already did.

**You can see what is going on.** A photo linked on another device names that device ("From Eric's MacBook"), and "Export media report" on the Media Storage page writes where every photo stands on this device. If a photo still shows as a placeholder after this update, attach that report from the device showing it to a bug report.

### Log a dive for a buddy, plan dives, fill them from a download

A buddy can now be linked to one of your local diver profiles through a "Linked profile" picker on the buddy page (the app suggests it once when the name or email matches exactly one profile). Saving a dive with a linked buddy offers to log the same dive in that profile as a planned sibling carrying the shared facts: times, site, conditions, weather, types, tags and the whole buddy group with roles. The Buddies card lists siblings under "Logged with", a detail-page action offers the mirror later, and the snackbar can undo it.

Planned dives get a full lifecycle of their own: "Plan a dive" in the add sheet, a planned switch on the edit page, a banner with "Mark as logged" on the detail page and a "Planned" chip in the lists. Planned dives stay unnumbered until they are logged. When you then download from a dive computer, the review pairs otherwise unmatched downloads with same-day planned dives of the target profile and pre-selects "Fill planned dive", with a picker to change the target or import as new. The fill takes the measured facts from the computer, keeps the plan's human facts, matches tanks by transmitter serial and then by gas mix, numbers the dive, and the summary can undo it. A failed fill imports as a new dive so a download is never lost.

### Import a Diving Log or DiveLogDT logbook

Divers migrating from DiveLogDT via DL7 lost every site, buddy, equipment item, weight, tank and trip, because DL7 carries none of them. Submersion now imports the SQLite logbook that DiveLogDT exports directly, which is the same format Diving Log uses on Windows, so one importer serves both. A generic SQLite file that turns out to be a Diving Log logbook is recognised automatically. It brings across dives, profiles, tanks, weights, sites with their coordinates, buddies, equipment, trips, dive centers, dive types, certifications, marine life sightings and photos. Gear has no type in this format, so it is typed from its name, and anything unplaced lands on Other where the Data Tools retype page reaches it. A dive whose site the file does not carry is counted into the import summary rather than silently losing its site. Validated against a real 444-dive logbook.

### Tags on equipment

The shared tag list now reaches equipment. A tag can be scoped to equipment under Settings > Manage > Tags ("Use for equipment"), and the equipment edit page gains a Tags field after Notes. The detail page shows an item's tags as chips, tapping one opens the list filtered to that tag, the equipment list shows up to three chips per tile and gains a Tags table column, a Tags group in the filter panel and search by tag name, and the selection bar gains "Edit tags" for bulk editing with Undo. Equipment tags sync between devices and travel through the full UDDF backup and the equipment CSV, which gains a Tags column.

### Every O2 cell on the chart, on a cleaner chart

Per-cell CCR O2 readings reached the profile chart only as tooltip rows, and the averaged value is exactly what hides a failing cell. Each cell is now drawn as its own curve behind the existing "O2 cells" toggle. Where a dive carries both a calibrated ppO2 and raw millivolts (Shearwater), a ppO2/mV switch on the legend row picks the unit, ppO2 by default, and the choice is remembered per device. The cell agreement rug now also works for dives that log only ppO2, such as Subsurface and UDDF imports, and the "O2 cells" chip is offered for either unit.

The chart itself was cleaned up. Lines that visually merged when several secondary series were active are now 1px and solid, ascent rate is drawn as bars coloured by speed category, and the GF%, surface GF%, TTS, ppO2, ppN2, ppHe and gas density axes stretch to the dive's actual maximum instead of clipping at a fixed cap. In the fullscreen view the docked readout card is replaced by a tooltip that follows the cursor and the playback marker, and scrubbing now lines up with the minimap and reaches the last sample. Hovering an O2 cell highlights only that cell.

### Service status wherever gear appears

The service overdue and due-soon signal used to live only inside the equipment feature. It now shows wherever current gear is chosen or listed: the gear tree on dive edit, the equipment and set pickers, the regulator dropdown, the component picker, "Part of" and "Installed in" rows, the pre-dive start sheet, the trip scrubber margin card, the incident gear picker, the dive computer's linked-gear row, the weight planner and dive planner gear chips. Due soon now says what is due and when rather than relying on an amber colour, and an assembly flags an overdue part inside it by name. Logged past dives and exports stay clean, so a red mark on a 2019 dive never reads as a claim about that dive.

The home strip now shows at most one gear chip per severity: a single item keeps its deep link ("Regulator service overdue"), while several become a count ("4 items overdue for service") that opens the equipment list filtered to exactly those items; the equipment filter sheet gains Overdue and Due Soon beside Service Due. The maintenance history dialog lists only the service types with a schedule on that item (with a toggle for all), shows the five most recent entries with "Show N more", and each service clock reads its accrued usage beside the remaining value, for example "61 dives, 59 of 120 dives left". The pre-dive checklist shows every configured clock on a gear row with its current value, not only the overdue ones, and links a completed check to your actual next dive by date rather than a fixed time window.

### Tell identical gear apart

Several identical pieces of gear could not be told apart in any list. The cylinder-only "Identifier" attribute is now available on every equipment type, so a mask, a light or a pair of weight pouches can carry a short mark such as "P2". Gear lists show brand and model, then the identifier, and when two rows still read the same each gains the first detail that differs (serial number, then size, then purchase date). This applies to the Add Equipment picker, the gear list on dive detail and edit, the equipment list and search, set detail and edit, and the component picker.

An equipment set can also now apply itself: a set that includes a dive computer as a member, with the new "Apply when this set's computer is imported" switch turned on in its edit page, is applied in full to every dive imported from that computer, whether by download, file import, HealthKit or re-download.

### Remember what worked with an operator's rental gear

Dive centers can hold rental gear notes: gear type, the operator's label, size, a worked-or-avoid verdict, an optional lead adjustment, an optional true tank capacity, free text and the dive it was noticed on. In the dive edit form, picking a dive center shows "Last time at {center}" with the lead, weighting feedback and tanks of your most recent other dive there plus the notes; "Apply last dive" copies those weights and tanks into the form, and "Add rental note" opens the note sheet. The dive center detail page gains a "Rental gear" section grouped by gear type.

### Site Details and the Sites list

Site Details now opens like Dive Center Details: the map comes first when the site has coordinates (bathymetry, feature markers and fullscreen included), followed by a pinned header with the site's name, rating, location line and chips for site types, difficulty and water type. The detailed site card in the Sites list drops the four personal aggregates (Last dived, Your max, Your avg, Avg time) and the difficulty chip by default, so the site's name, location, depth range and dive count come first; anyone who customised the card keeps their choice. The sites filter's free-text Country and Region fields are now searchable dropdowns fed by the countries and regions already in your sites, with the region list narrowed to the chosen country. The site's access card lists parking first, then access notes, entry and exit method and mooring, each label above its value.

### 3D terrain that sharpens as you zoom

The 3D dive site terrain view now loads finer terrain as you zoom in: beyond the always-loaded 8 km base there are four stages down to a 1 km super-fine patch, each with its own contours, steep-wall highlight, imagery drape and hover picking, and a quiet hint when the source has no more detail to give. Depth is now exaggerated automatically from the site's own footprint (1x to 8x) so a real site no longer renders nearly flat, with a per-site override in the terrain-appearance sheet and a read-out next to the compass. A new "3D Maps" page under Settings > Data > Storage manages the cached terrain: refresh or clear the swissBATHY3D tiles, reset the other providers, or reload map data for every known site with a size estimate, progress and cancel. Swiss lake sites that froze, crashed or reported "no bathymetry data" now look up each lake once instead of once per tile, so a second site on the same lake opens about six times faster and one transient swisstopo failure no longer fails the whole view.

### Sync fixes beyond media

Deleting a diver on one device now removes their whole library on every other device (dives, trips, sites, gear, buddies and tags), where before it left it ownerless and visible to every diver there. A tag or type added to the same dive on two devices while apart now stays deleted once either device removes it, and narrowing a tag's scope drops its links everywhere. Choosing "Keep Remote" on a deletion conflict no longer fails when something local still references the record, such as a saved plan built from the dive.

### Real ways back in when the dive log cannot be read

When the dive log could not be read at startup on a fresh install, the failure screen offered only an empty backup folder and Close, even when a good dive log sat in iCloud Drive. The screen now has an "Other ways back in" section: "Use a dive log in another folder" (pick a folder, see how many dives and sites it holds, then confirm), "Restore from a backup file" (validated before it replaces anything), and "Start with an empty dive log" (the damaged database is moved aside, never deleted). The "Your Data Is Newer Than This App" screen was rewritten from a wall of text into a short list of causes and a clear stack of routes.

### Navigation and profiles

A new "Show labels" switch under Settings > Appearance > Navigation layout hides or shows labels on the phone bottom bar and the desktop rail, and the bottom bar now shows as many primary slots as the width allows instead of a fixed three. When the rail hides its labels, hovering or long-pressing an icon names it in a tooltip. Once two or more diver profiles exist, the dashboard hero header leads with the active diver's avatar; tapping it opens the diver switcher, which gains a pinned "Add New Diver" row.

### Smaller additions

- **The certification wallet is a vertical grid** showing several cards at once, each with its own action row, instead of paging sideways one card at a time; the detail page stacks the front and back photos full-width and uncropped.
- **Favourite a buddy from the Dive Partners list** or the buddy detail page; favourites pin to the top of the list regardless of sort.
- **The trip story's pinned map and day header are one band** that shrinks as you scroll, so permanent chrome drops from about a third of a phone screen to a strip, and the day cross-fades as you pass each heading. In the Dives list a trip can now be collapsed while one of its dives is open, and the header loses its accent stripe.
- **Subsurface imports classify dives from Subsurface's own tags** (cave, cavern, wreck, boat, shore, drift, deep, ice, altitude, night, student), suggest a site type from place tags, and keep a sidemount role on sidemount cylinders.
- **Unrecognised dive types are kept**, with your own spelling, instead of being silently recorded as Recreational; "Cenote", "Sidemount", "CCR" and the like become custom types that appear in the picker and filters.
- **Automatic deco-based dive type classification is removed.** A download or import with no explicit dive type is Recreational, as it was before the heuristic was added; a type carried by the file still wins.
- **Site features travel through UDDF and the sites CSV**, which was also lossy for most of a site and now carries minimum depth, difficulty, altitude, city, island, body of water, hazards, access notes, mooring number, parking, exit method, types and tags.
- **Importers keep a dive's location when it has no named site.** Shearwater Cloud, CSV, MacDive, UDDF, DAN DL7 and the sites CSV used to discard coordinates for lack of a name; a nameless site is now named from its coordinates. A pre-4.5 Subsurface export's inline sites are read too.
- **Choosing "Oxygen Supply" as a cylinder's role pre-fills 100% O2**, and a tank a Shearwater tags as oxygen imports as 100% O2 instead of the diluent's mix.
- **Profile image export moves into the Export sheet** as "Profile as Image", above "Page as Image"; the share icon and the duplicate "Replan this dive" icon leave the chart header.
- **Equipment Details' overflow menu drops Retire and Reactivate**, which duplicated the Status field on the Edit page, and the wide-layout Equipment/Sets toggle becomes the header title.
- **The trimix mixer** gets a batch of fixes from the Android beta: ambient pressure in your pressure unit, inline errors for an unreadable decimal separator, flexible unit-labelled tables that fit narrow screens, the cylinder size on each billed gas line, and deletable archived invoices.
- **Translations regain their accents.** Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Dutch and Hungarian strings that had been written without diacritics (over 5,000 in total) are spelled correctly, and in French and Portuguese a count of zero now reads "0" instead of "1" across about 250 strings.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

**Imports and exports**

- A mistyped O2 or He percentage (wrong decimal separator, a stray letter) was silently replaced by air, dropping the helium from a trimix. The dive log tank editor now keeps the last good mix and shows "Enter a valid number", and the planner refuses to save until it is fixed.
- A Submersion dives CSV opened and saved in a spreadsheet failed to import because the spreadsheet rewrote the date column. Those date spellings are now read, and unreadable rows are listed by number.
- The dive type keyword "ice" matched inside "Practice", "Service dive" and "Novice", recording ordinary dives as ice dives. It is now matched as a whole word.
- Two files in one multi-file import that held the same site lost any tags or site types only the later file gave it.
- Running "Re-analyze raw data" on a dive from a computer without a transmitter wiped the tank pressures you had typed by hand. Pressures already wiped are not restored.
- After downloading a CCR dive, the CCR panel's "Diluent Gas" showed the Air default while the Diluent cylinder beside it was right, and editing any other CCR field saved that default. The diluent now comes from the resolved Diluent cylinder on download and re-parse.
- UDDF export, the PADI and Detailed PDF templates and the table view printed a blank water type and entry method for dives that inherited them from the site.
- The trip report and course training log PDFs always printed metric depth and temperature. The Detailed logbook failed with "Widget won't fit into the page" when a dive's notes, a custom field or a sighting note ran taller than a page, and the trip report and training log printed a bare "Notes:" label in the same case. All now continue onto the next page.

**Dive log and charts**

- Combining two dives split by a surface interval kept every tank, so each cylinder appeared twice. Combine now carries one tank per physical cylinder with both halves' pressure logs; dives combined before this fix keep their duplicate tanks.
- Assigning a wreck site to a dive never marked it as a wreck dive. Assigning a site in the editor now adds the dive types its site types stand for; existing dives are not rewritten.
- The Details card showed no visibility row for any dive logged since measured visibility arrived, even though the edit page had the value. Typing unreadable text into the visibility field saved a measured 0 m, which put the dive in the "Poor" band; it now saves as unknown.
- When a part was added to an assembly already on past dives and you answered "From now on", those dives showed "4 components" for a 9-part regulator with no way to fix it. The gear tree now reads "4 of 9 components" and the edit page offers "Add missing parts".
- After applying an equipment set and swapping one item by hand, the hand-added gear always sorted after the set's gear. All of a dive's gear now sorts as one list, with the sets shown as chips above it, on the detail page, the edit page and the Detailed PDF.
- The detailed dive card's stat row overflowed in a narrow master pane, and the OC/CCR/SCR mode badge crowded the title line; it now sits on the dive-type badge row.
- The data quality pressure check raised false "end pressure mismatch" findings when the sensor kept logging after surfacing, and false start mismatches when a transmitter started logging late. Both are now silent unless the samples can corroborate them.
- The hidden inline chart on the dive detail page rebuilt itself about 31 times a second behind fullscreen playback; it no longer listens to playback.

**Equipment**

- Tapping "Manage service types" from the Add Service Record dialog opened the page hidden behind the dialog. It now opens on top and returns to the dialog with your input intact.
- The total service cost row repeated the currency twice for currencies whose symbol is the bare code (CHF) and could overflow off-screen.
- The pre-dive checklist evaluated gear service clocks once per row (291 database statements for a 20-row session); it now runs about a dozen regardless of row count.

**Statistics and planner**

- The Water type and Entry method charts dropped dives with no value, so every percentage was a share of only the dives that had one; they now show a "Not recorded" segment. The Solo vs Buddy chart counted every dive with no buddy as solo, and counted placeholders such as "None" or "n/a" as a buddy; a dive is now solo only when your own role on it is Solo, and placeholders read as no buddy.
- With cubic feet selected, the planner showed a tank size multiplied by 200 on the tank chip (80 cuft became 16000) and pre-filled the AL80 as "0.4". Cubic feet are now read as rated gas capacity at the tank's working pressure.
- Saving a dive plan failed, losing the whole plan, when the site it named, the dive it was planned from or the dive it was linked to had been deleted meanwhile. The save now drops only the dead reference.

**Sites, trips and buddies**

- On the site and dive center edit forms every coordinate problem showed "Invalid latitude", even when the longitude was wrong, and a decimal comma typed into Longitude was read as a latitude-longitude pair. Each axis now reports its own error and accepts a decimal comma.
- Name sorts compared raw characters, so every capitalised name sorted before every lowercase one and "Écueil" sorted after "z". Sorting now ignores case and accents across sites, trips, tags, buddies, dive centers, equipment, species and every other named list.
- Deleting a dive center that a dive used, or a site that a dive or plan used, failed with a database error; deleting a diver failed whenever they owned a service kind, preset, template, plan or pre-dive session; deleting a dive failed when a plan had been built from it. All now succeed, the confirmation says how many dives will be left without a site, and merging sites moves plans to the survivor.
- Buddy search results showed a wrong dive count for every hit, and the "Add buddy" sheet could show a stale count while a link arrived through sync.
- The trip edit page's duration label and the upcoming-trip countdown counted elapsed hours, so a daylight-saving change lost a day; the countdown was also hardcoded English and read "In 1 days". Both count calendar days now, the countdown is translated, and the start day reads "Starting today". The tide table's "Tomorrow" label had the same daylight-saving flaw.
- The floating "Add Tag" button covered the edit button of the last tag under Settings > Manage > Tags; the same fix applies to every Manage list.
- A tag's colour in Settings did not match the colour its chips showed. Chips now resolve the tint to one opaque value so a tag reads the same on a selected row, and the Settings swatches, merge sheet and list rows show the chip a tag actually displays.
- In the light theme, status chips were filled with a pastel so close to the page that they read as a smudge. Alert chips are now a solid fill with a reversed label, while warn and OK keep a pale wash so an overdue regulator still stands out from a no-fly timer.

**Photos**

- Photos selected in the media picker for one site or dive stayed ticked the next time the picker opened for another. Each session now starts empty.

**Platform and startup**

- Copy Diagnostics named every Windows 11 machine "Windows 10".
- On Android, "Choose from Contacts" had never worked because the contacts permission was never declared. It works now, and a permanent denial offers "Open Settings".
- Restoring a backup could abort before it reached the file you picked, because pruning old backups hit one that could not be deleted (a locked network share, a folder outside the sandbox). Pruning is now best-effort and the restore carries on.
- The "Finish setting up this device" card in Settings updated on the first change and then ignored every later one until Settings was reopened.
- The Bluetooth scan tab's empty state overflowed in a short window; it now scrolls like the USB tab.

## ⚠️ Upgrade notes

- **The database upgrades from schema 218 to 226.** The migration runs once on first launch and the app takes an automatic backup beforehand, so there is nothing you need to do first.
- **Update every device in your sync library.** This release raises the sync compatibility floor again, so a device still on v1.7.10 or earlier stops receiving changes from an updated device until it updates too. Its own changes still arrive on the updated device, so nothing is lost, but the older device falls behind until you update it.
- **Every existing tag starts with equipment use switched off.** To use one on gear, turn on "Use for equipment" under Settings > Manage > Tags.
- **Deco dives are no longer auto-tagged Technical on import.** Set the type yourself if you relied on that; dives already tagged by the old heuristic are unchanged.
- **Solo vs Buddy counts a dive as solo only when your role on it is Solo.** Dives with no buddy and no Solo role move to "Not recorded" until you set the role on them (the "Me" chip in the buddy picker).
- **The Site Details map is no longer a configurable card**, so it leaves the section settings and anyone who had hidden it sees it again. The default site list card drops the personal stat fields and difficulty chip; a card you customised is kept, and all five fields can be added back.
- **Site types are added to a dive only when a site is assigned from now on**; existing dives keep their types. Equipment sets apply themselves on import only after you turn on "Apply when this set's computer is imported" on each set.
- **Only new imports benefit from the importer fixes** (unrecognised dive types, the ice keyword, locations without a named site, pre-4.5 Subsurface sites, Subsurface tag classification, oxygen tank mixes). Re-import the affected files to recover them; dives already imported are not relabelled.
- **UDDF and sites CSV files exported before this release carry no site features.** Export again if you keep them as an archive. The sites CSV's new columns are appended after the historical twelve.
- **Retire and Reactivate move to the Status field on the equipment Edit page.** Profile image export moves to the Export sheet, and the fullscreen readout card and its settings are replaced by the cursor tooltip.
- **The swissBATHY3D refresh action moves** from Settings > Appearance to Settings > Data > Storage > 3D Maps.
- **Deleting a diver now also removes the templates, presets, plans, pre-dive sessions and media they owned**, on every synced device. Libraries and media already orphaned by an earlier deletion are not repaired.
- **Older gallery links gain their iCloud identifier only after a successful sync** on a device with full photo access, and photos already marked missing by an earlier wrong verdict are not repaired automatically.
- **Android now asks for the contacts permission** the first time you use "Choose from Contacts".
- **The per-cell O2 unit choice is a device-local preference**, not synced.
- **No minimum operating system change** on macOS, Windows, iOS, Android or Linux.
