# 🎉 Submersion v1.8.6.9762 Release Notes

v1.8.6 is a small follow-up to v1.8.5. It fixes an Android crash loop when a dive video uploads to a cloud media store, and it makes the planner's stop times readable while previewing a contingency, on desktop and on phones. There is no database change in this release.

---

## ✨ New and improved

### Planner stop tags follow the contingency preview

While a contingency was previewed (for example +5 m with EAN50 lost), the stop tags on the plan chart still described the base plan, even though the deco table and the stats beside them showed the preview. The tags now sit on the dashed preview profile and show how much each stop changed, as a second pill such as `+3` or `−2`. A stop the base plan does not have is drawn in the warning colour, so the cost of a contingency reads straight off the chart. Without a preview, nothing changes.

### Stop list on phones

On a phone the chart was too narrow for its stop tags, which covered each other and the TTS, Deco and CNS figures, worst on a deep plan with many stops. The phone chart now lists the stops under CNS instead, shallowest first, with each stop's change in red while a contingency is previewed and new stops in the warning colour. Six rows show at once and the rest scroll, so a trimix plan with a dozen stops from 40 m and deeper stays readable. Desktop and the full-screen chart keep their tags.

---

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- **Android: crash after saving a dive with gallery videos, then on every launch.** With a cloud media store set up, uploading a dive video read the whole file into memory and ran the app out of it, so it crashed after the dive was saved and again a second or two after every relaunch while the upload was still queued. Videos are now read as files, as they already were on iPhone, iPad and Mac, so memory stays flat whatever the video's size. After updating, the queued upload simply finishes.

---

## ⚠️ Upgrade notes

- **No database change:** the schema stays at 382 and the sync compatibility floor at 381, so v1.8.5 and v1.8.6 devices sync with each other and can update in any order.
- **If you denied "Photos and videos" access** to get the Android app to open, you can grant it again after updating.
- **No minimum operating system change** on macOS, Windows, iOS, Android or Linux.
