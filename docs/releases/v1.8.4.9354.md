# 🎉 Submersion v1.8.4.9354 Release Notes

v1.8.4 brings 259 merged changes since v1.8.3. The headlines are sync between your own devices over Bluetooth, buddy card swaps, planning a whole day of repetitive dives, and comparing a dive against its plan and against what-if alternatives. Every change to your data now lands in a change log you can revert from, one search field finds any record and understands plain words, notes take Markdown formatting and can pin a passage to a moment in the dive, and a sample logbook lets new users explore before entering their own dives. The planner gains per-O2-band deco limits, a hypercapnia bailout phase and a worst-case bailout marker, trips can hold side trips and several checklists, gear can be lent to a buddy, and photos can be tagged with the people in them. A long run of correctness work means many figures change, so please read "Numbers that change" in the upgrade notes. This release upgrades the database (schema 318 to 346) and raises the sync compatibility floor, so please read the upgrade notes at the end.

---

## ✨ New and improved

### Sync between your own devices over Bluetooth

Nearby sync lets two of your own Submersion devices sync their logbook directly over Bluetooth, with no network, account or server, alongside any cloud backend. Settings > Data > Sync between devices gains a Nearby devices block with a switch, your paired devices and Pair a device, which shows a six-digit code on both screens to confirm. A home chip appears when a paired device needs attention. Nearby sync runs only while the app is in the foreground.

### Swap buddy cards

"Swap with a buddy" on the Buddies list exchanges buddy profiles with another diver offline: over Bluetooth on every platform, by holding two iPhones together (iPhone 11 and later), with a QR code, an Android NFC tap, an NFC sticker or a shared .subbuddy file. You choose what your card shares (never medical, insurance or emergency details), and every card you receive opens a preview before anything is saved. Buddy and diver pages gain a social handle field.

### Plan a whole day of repetitive dives

The planner can now hold a series of dives. Add dives after the first, move between them on a series bar above the profile, and set each surface interval, start time and site. Each dive starts from what the earlier ones left behind (tissue loading, CNS, a rolling 24-hour OTU total and remaining tank pressures), and the series is checked for short surface intervals, overlapping starts, starting with deco owed and reverse profiles. Settings and tanks are shared across the series unless a dive sets its own. A series can follow a logged dive ("Plan repetitive dive" in the dive menu, or a one-tap prompt after a recent dive), and it saves, shares and reopens as one plan. "Create planned dives" replaces Convert to Dive and puts every dive of the series in the log as planned dives with full profiles; once you have dived one, the planner opens on the actual dive with the plan ghosted, and the next dive carries on from what you really did.

### Compare: plan, as dived and what-if

"Compare..." on a dive's menu (it replaces "Replan this dive") branches a logged dive at any moment, lets you change decisions (switch gas, lose a cylinder, shift the ascent, gradient factors, share gas, bail out) and compares that alternate timeline against what happened: deco, tissues, CNS and OTU, gas and buoyancy. A saved plan can be linked to the dive it became, and dive details gain a Compare section showing Planned, As dived and What-if together with one chart and a table. Scenarios can be saved, shared as a PDF slate, an image or a .sublab file, and opened in the planner.

### A change log you can revert from

Settings > Data > Change log records the old and new value of every field of your data, whatever changed it: edits, sync, imports, downloads, bulk edits and deletes. A filterable feed and a History item on record pages let you revert one field, an entry or a whole bulk action, restore deleted records with everything under them, or undo an import. The log is kept per device and not synced (a revert is an ordinary edit, so it does sync), retention can be 3, 6 or 12 months or forever, and credentials are never logged.

### Search everything, in plain words

A new Search destination (above Settings, or under More on phones, and Cmd/Ctrl+K) finds dives, sites, trips, equipment and sets, buddies, dive centers, certifications, courses, species, documents, saved plans, GPS tracks and underwater routes from one field, grouped by type. Every list's search uses the same field, with a "Search everything for..." row beneath it. The dive search understands plain words: "ressel in october 2018 with mike" finds the dive, matching gear, trips, species, courses and computers, values like nitrox or ean32, and date phrases such as "last year" or "2018 to 2020" in all 11 app languages. Typing a whole dive number filters to that dive, and you can search for empty fields with phrases like "Trip is not set".

### Formatted notes and annotations

Notes and descriptions everywhere (dives, sites, trips, buddies, dive centers, certifications, courses, species, documents, equipment and more) can be formatted with Markdown: headings, bold, italic, lists, task lists, quotes, tables and links. Editors gain a small formatting bar and a Preview toggle, and PDF exports print the formatting. Annotations tie a passage of a dive's notes to a moment in the dive: amber markers on the profile, placed from the Dive Profile card, by right-clicking the chart or from the edit page, which open the words, time and depth and jump to the passage in Notes.

### Explore with sample data

New users can try Submersion on a realistic six-year sample logbook before entering their own: 700 dives from local diving and trips around the world, with buddies, gear and service history, photos, scooter routes and boat tracks. Start it from "Explore with sample data" on the welcome screen or at the end of Settings > Data > Advanced. A strip on every screen shows you are exploring, and Exit demo puts your own logbook and preferences back exactly as they were. The bundled site catalogue also gains 55 curated sites.

### Agent access on desktop

Desktop builds can run an optional Model Context Protocol server, under Settings > Data > Advanced > Data tools > Agent access, so an AI agent on the same computer can read and, with your confirmation, change your logbook. It listens on this computer only, needs a token, and refuses in demo mode, while the app is locked and during a restore. Every write first shows a preview in your units of what it will change, and lands in the change log as an Agent change you can revert. Deleting or merging a diver profile, adding document pages and attaching media stay in the app. It is off by default; see the new Agent access page in the user guide.

### Planner

- **Per-O2-band deco limits:** separate deco ppO2 limits for gases up to 28%, 28 to 45% and 45 to 99% O2, and an explicit MOD for oxygen, under Settings > Safety > Decompression and per plan. Off by default.
- **Hypercapnia bailout phase:** an optional elevated breathing rate for a set time at the start of an open-circuit bailout. The bailout calculation and Bailout chart view now work for SCR plans too.
- **Worst-case bailout point:** loop plans with a bailout cylinder mark it on the chart with a "Worst case" pill showing runtime, depth, bailout TTS and gas required against available.
- **Warnings on the profile:** each plan warning shows as a sign on the chart where it starts, linked to its rows in the warnings list.
- Editing a plan is much smoother: the range table, bailout and contingency tables calculate in the background and mark older figures as "Updating".
- The chart's position line locks onto waypoints and stop arrivals instead of stopping short of them.

### Trips

Select dives in the dive list and choose "New Trip from Selection" to open a trip form prefilled from those dives. Trips can be side trips, "Part of" a larger trip that nests them and counts their dives. A new trip made from the dive editor or bulk edit is prefilled too, and a trip stretches its dates when a dive lands outside them. A trip can carry several named checklists, such as "Dive equipment" and "Clothing", each with its own sections and progress. On a trip's Gear tab, From my equipment picks several items at once.

### Equipment

- **Lend gear to a buddy** from the Move sheet or the buddy's page; a "Gear with {name}" card tracks it and Returned puts each item back where it was.
- **Dive center places:** a new place type records gear left at a dive center, and that center's page lists it under Your gear here.
- **Reports:** an Equipment inventory PDF and a Service report PDF under Transfer > Export, plus Cost of ownership, Idle gear and an expanded Most used gear in Insights > Equipment.
- **Auto-connect known computers:** while Transfer > Dive Computers is open, Submersion opens a known Bluetooth computer's download page when it comes in range. On by default, with a per-computer opt-out. Dive Computers can also be pinned as a navigation shortcut.
- A near-miss can be linked to the gear check-in behind it, the Details card shows total Dive Time, long type groups show three items and "Show N more", and gear pickers hide an assembly's components unless you ask for them.
- Cylinder tags, gear tags and buddy card stickers can use NFC Forum Type 5 tags such as NXP ICODE SLIX.

### Dive log

- **Edit gas switches:** dives with two or more cylinders gain a Gas switches list in the editor, with suggested times from transmitter pressure drops and a profile picker for the exact moment. Saving recalculates deco, tissues, CNS, OTU and SAC, and re-parse keeps your list.
- The Cylinders card draws each cylinder as a tank figure showing gas left and used, and each cylinder's MOD follows its role (a deco EAN50 shows 22 m, not 18 m).
- A new Twin Set cylinder role; SAC and gas use treat it like back gas.
- CCR dives show O2 and diluent use rates per minute of loop time, and Gas statistics gain a Rebreather gas use card.
- The Gas consumption by segment card shows time, phase and gas together on one readable chart.
- The profile editor's Trim and Outliers modes become one Cleanup mode, which can also delete a whole profile revision.
- Bulk edit can set the dive site, serial and card numbers can be copied with a long press, and a ceiling drawn from Submersion's own calculation is labelled "Ceiling (calc)".

### Photos and videos

Tag yourself and your buddies in photos and videos: "Tag people" in the viewer marks a spot on a photo or a paused video frame. People show beside species, a buddy's page gains a Photos and videos card, and the library filter gains People, so "Photos of me" can be a smart album. Photos are now matched to dives by their camera capture time, so linking photos taken in a different time zone from your phone finds them. On iOS and Android, caches move to the system cache folder, so Android's Clear cache removes them and they stay out of device backups. Site and equipment media get their own groups in the By dive view, and the filter gains a Source row.

### Insights

Every chart that counts dives by category now opens the dives behind each bar or slice, including "Not recorded". A selector at the top of Insights switches between All dives and saved queries used as groups, such as cold-water drysuit dives against warm-water dives. A new Calendar Days card on Time Patterns shows dives on each calendar date across all years. A new First day of week setting (Settings > Units > Time & Date) drives the Day of Week chart, weekly trends and date pickers, and the Units page is regrouped. A buddy can be left out of buddy rankings, for example an "Unknown" buddy.

### Lists, tables and navigation

Every list reopens the way you left it: sort, grouping and view mode for Dives, Saved Dive Plans, Trips, Courses, Buddies, Dive Centers, Dive Sites, Certifications and Equipment are kept per diver and sync to your other devices. A new Table editor under Settings > Data > Advanced > Data tools edits dives, sites and dive centers like a spreadsheet, including pasting a block from Excel, and saves everything as one undoable change (it needs a window at least 800 px wide). Messages at the bottom of the screen now close on their own, with "Dismiss all" when several are waiting.

### Tracks and maps

A dead-reckoned underwater track (Suunto Nautic S, Seacraft ENC) can be scaled against the map from 25% to 400%. Seacraft ENC tracks are anchored on the dive's first sample, so they no longer land kilometres away; the original file is kept, console markers are shown, re-importing a file offers to update the track, and "Export as CSV" is new. An underwater track's console battery voltage has its own recording-device card, and Tracks rows are cards that match the dive list.

### Backups and sync

Any backup in the history can be shared or saved elsewhere, and each row names the device that made it. When a cloud account stops working, Data health, the Sync status card and Backups now offer "Sign in again" rather than reading as offline. Heavy sync work runs in the background, so scrolling no longer stutters during a large sync. The Settings > About page gains a User Guide link, and the user guide gains how-to guides and "Moving from" guides for MacDive, Subsurface, Shearwater Cloud, Diving Log, Garmin Connect, Suunto, divelogs.de and DAN DiveCloud.

### Faster on large logbooks

Opening long profiles, the dive detail page, trips, the home dashboard, documents, the dive picker, Compare in 3D, Data Quality scans, the query editor and large UDDF and Subsurface imports are all noticeably faster, and imports no longer freeze the wizard while a big file is read.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

**Dive computers**

- On Windows, a Shearwater Petrel 2 that was found but would not download now retries Bluetooth LE discovery and falls back to Bluetooth Classic. A failed Windows Bluetooth connection now explains why instead of "Failed to connect to device", and Windows no longer drops discovery, progress, PIN requests or errors.
- Downloads now store the surface pressure the computer measured (Shearwater, Mares Icon HD, Suunto EON Steel, OSTC, Cressi Goa, Divesoft, Ratio and others). Re-parse existing dives to pick it up.
- A dive already logged from another computer often failed to consolidate, because the match compared runtime with bottom time.
- On a CCR dive with no logged gas switch, such as a Perdix in CC/BO mode, the dive started on the first listed cylinder (often a bailout) instead of the diluent.
- Re-parsing a dive with a manual profile edit reset its maximum and average depth to the original recording's.
- A calculated safety stop was drawn on the last deco stop of a logged deco dive.
- A transmitter serial could not be given to an item made by hand once a download had created a placeholder for it.

**Dive log and analysis**

- Mean depth, GTR and rolling SAC averaged samples equally, so uneven sample intervals skewed them; they are now weighted by time.
- Logged-dive ppO2, CNS, OTU and SAC ignored the dive's altitude, surface pressure and water type, so an altitude dive could show a critical exposure it never had.
- The oxygen card reported an averaged maximum ppO2, hiding a peak past the critical limit.
- A dive with no profile reset the next dive's residual CNS to 0%, and in a logbook shared by several divers, residual CNS, OTU and tissues could include another diver's dives.
- Tissues between repetitive altitude dives were off-gassed at sea-level pressure, and the live CNS/OTU card gained or lost an hour across a daylight saving change.
- A saved plan that was never dived counted as a dive for no-fly, surface intervals and residual loading once its time passed.
- Statistics, records, SAC and the PDF logbook used bottom time as a stand-in for runtime.
- Per-cylinder SAC counted only the first breathing period of a cylinder you went back to, and a range breathed only from a stage cylinder lost its SAC.
- The profile's right axis showed wrong, upside-down labels on Mean Depth, and overlaid computers' temperatures and tank pressures did not share one scale.
- The profile editor's range tools were always disabled, outliers were interpolated by sample position rather than time, and Draw mode in feet saved profiles 3.28 times too deep.
- Range statistics ignored time between a handle and its nearest sample, and SAC changed when a selection ran past the pressure readings.
- Saving only a note on an imported dive rounded its times to whole minutes.
- Combining dives took temperature, end CNS and OTU from the first segment only; affected dives are repaired on upgrade.
- Undo of a bulk delete, a bulk tank edit or a consolidation lost recordings, pressure series, gas switches, routes or later route edits.
- Splitting a source off a dive left its profile on the old dive's timeline, and swapping the primary source kept the old runtime.
- Data source times were shown shifted by the device's time zone.
- Typing a dive number and pressing Enter opened the dive but never scrolled the list to it.

**Planner and calculators**

- The automatic ascent's travel was left out of CNS and OTU totals, and ascent gas was charged to the wrong cylinder at switches.
- ppO2, CNS, OTU and gas figures ignored the plan's water type and altitude.
- SCR and pSCR plans computed their ascent as open circuit, understating deco.
- A CCR ramp across the setpoint switch depth breathed one setpoint for the whole leg.
- A rebreather plan deeper than every bailout gas's MOD reported its bailout as sufficient, and a computed ascent on hypoxic gas was not flagged.
- DPV mission budgets left out travel between stops, problem-solving time and air breaks, so some exits looked survivable when they were not.
- The turn pressure could fall below the plan's own rock-bottom minimum gas.
- Open-circuit legs in a CCR plan used no gas.
- The Trimix blender refused feasible top-ups across two temperatures and refused to fill an empty cylinder whose last mix held helium; its fill-order table now fits on phones.
- Exporting the slate of an empty plan produced a near-blank PDF.

**Imports, exports and backups**

- UDDF exports wrote bottom time as the dive's duration, lost a second computer when raw data was left out, dropped rebreather data, merged nearly equal gas mixes, and wrote setpoints in a unit other apps misread.
- Restoring a UDDF backup merged every computer's recording into one source, lost which computer logged each gas switch, and moved tank pressure readings. Only backups made from now on carry the fixes.
- CSV and Excel exports could write a mix totalling 101%; such files now import with a notice.
- Importing two sites with the same name in different places, such as two Blue Holes, merged them into one.
- CSV import dropped a cylinder with a blank size, along with its pressures and gas.
- A MacDive import counted its buddies twice, and UDDF "Link to existing" on a duplicate dive center left the dive with no center.
- Bulk "fetch conditions" stored the fetch time in the wrong unit; affected dives are repaired on upgrade.
- The home backup chip ignored the pre-upgrade backup the app had just made.

**Sync and accounts**

- Google Drive failed with "Could not read Google Drive" or a raw 403 when its box was left unticked on Google's consent page; you are now asked to sign in again with it ticked.
- Tapping Choose an account on Backups did nothing while the backup copy was off.
- One phone could appear several times in the sync device list after an interrupted upload, and removed devices stayed listed with a trash button that did nothing.
- A GPS track trimmed or split on one device kept its old line on other devices.

**Media, maps and layout**

- The media gallery jumped back to the top whenever something changed in the background, and Next and Previous in the viewer skipped around in By dive and Timeline views.
- "Write dive data to photo" was offered for media it could never write to.
- The overlay could show one cylinder's pressure beside another's gas.
- PDF attachment previews on dive sites were drawn small in an oversized box.
- The Decompression Obligation card on Insights could spin forever, and Insights could keep showing dives that no longer matched the filter.
- Insights showed "No dives logged yet" when a filter matched nothing or every dive was excluded from statistics.
- On phones, long notes pushed the editor under the app bar, and tall sheets reached under the status bar.
- Narrow phones wrapped dive coordinates mid-word, and Manage Tags squeezed tag names to an ellipsis.
- On macOS, closing the last window while the app was not responding could leave it running with no window.

## ⚠️ Upgrade notes

- **The database upgrades from schema 318 to 346.** The migration runs once on first launch and the app takes an automatic backup beforehand, so there is nothing you need to do first. Along the way it moves each trip's checklist into a default checklist named "Checklist", turns the equipment diver figure on for every set and diver, repairs weather fetch times stored in the wrong unit, recomputes temperature, CNS and OTU on combined dives, and moves each item's last service date to its newest service record.
- **Update every device in your sync library.** This release raises the sync compatibility floor from 308 to 336, so a device still on v1.8.3 or earlier stops receiving changes from an updated device until it updates too. Its own changes still arrive on the updated device, so nothing is lost, but the older device falls behind until you update it. Nearby sync and buddy card swaps also need this version on both devices.
- **Numbers that change:**
  - **Dive figures:** mean depth, GTR, rolling SAC and segment SAC are time-weighted. ppO2, CNS, OTU, SAC and RMV follow each dive's altitude, surface pressure and water type (about 0.5% in salt water, about 8% at 3000 m). Maximum ppO2 is the true peak, so some dives may now show a critical badge.
  - **Gas use:** "Gas used" follows the gas model setting (real gas by default), reading a few percent lower. Per-cylinder SAC changes on dives with repeated switches, and some dives gain a SAC they lacked. Displayed MODs follow each cylinder's role.
  - **Runtime-based statistics:** career hours, records, SAC and the PDF logbook use measured runtime. A dive with only a bottom time (hand-entered or from a paper log) still counts as a dive but adds no hours, so a large paper log reads lower until runtimes are filled in.
  - **Residual loading:** residual CNS and tissues carry past dives with no profile, count only the dive's own diver, and ignore plans that were never dived. Safety reviews recompute in the background after the upgrade.
  - **Gear:** usage counts, service clocks and Trips counts leave out planned dives. Tank configuration counts twin backmount only from the new Twin Set role; "Mark as twin sets" helps convert older dives.
  - **The week:** the Day of Week chart and weekly trends follow the new First day of week setting, which starts on Automatic.
- **Plans that change:** most open-circuit plans show higher CNS and OTU, SCR and pSCR plans show more deco, CCR plans with ramps across the setpoint switch depth change slightly, and gas figures follow the plan's altitude and water. Some DPV mission exits and bailout plans that passed before now report a shortfall or a bailout gas gap, and turn pressures rise where minimum gas exceeded the reserve. A salt-water plan on a gas at its exact limit (EAN40 at 30 m) can now go critical.
- **Behaviour that changes:**
  - **Notes and editors:** existing notes are now drawn as Markdown, the profile editor's Trim and Outliers modes are one Cleanup mode, and messages at the bottom of the screen close on their own.
  - **Planning and downloads:** "Replan this dive" is "Compare...", and "Create planned dives" replaces Convert to Dive. A download now fills a planned dive from its own day or the day before.
  - **Trips and sharing:** sharing a trip or site is changed on its edit page (the detail page's Shared with row is gone), trips stretch their dates to fit dives you add, and the trip Gear picker adds ticked items with Add N.
  - **Equipment:** the diver figure is on by default, even if you had turned it off, and Gear arrangement moved to Settings > Appearance > Dives. Auto-connect to known computers is on, and gear pickers hide assembly components behind a switch.
  - **Search:** the old full-screen list searches are replaced by the shared search field, the entry method search uses a site's method when the dive has none, and a dive with a stored zero weight now counts as no weight.
- **Only new data benefits** from the UDDF backup fixes (per-computer profiles, gas switch computers, tank pressure readings), the same-name site fix and photo matching by capture time. Surface pressure from the computer, and depths on dives with a manual profile edit, need a re-parse ("Re-parse all dives" on the computer's page). Not repaired automatically: profiles saved too deep from Draw mode in feet (revert them in the profile editor), dives split before this release, stored average depths, and duplicate rows already in the sync device list (remove them there). A UDDF file exported from this version carries rebreather setpoints that Submersion builds before v1.7.3 cannot read.
- **Platform notes:** Linux can scan for Nearby devices but cannot advertise itself, so pair from the other device. Agent access is desktop only.
- **Permissions:** Android asks for the Bluetooth advertise permission for Nearby sync. iOS asks for local network and Nearby Interaction access the first time you open the buddy card swap screen. No minimum operating system change on macOS, Windows, iOS, Android or Linux.
