# 🎉 Submersion v1.8.1.8710 Release Notes

v1.8.1 brings 304 merged changes since v1.8.0. The headline is search. One search row now sits over the Dives list and filters as you type, whether you give it a word, a query such as `depth > 30m`, or a plain sentence like "turtles below 20 m in Bonaire with viz over 20 m" that the on-device model turns into filters without your data leaving the phone. Every filter lives in one Refine panel, and the whole search can be saved and recalled. Trips get a page of their own with tabs, a cylinder board, a fill forecast, a gas record and packed gear. Every cylinder gains a passport with a printed QR label and an NFC tag. Gear can be shared between the profiles in a library and transferred between them, and a shared trip or site now belongs to the profile that made it. Underwater tracks from a Seacraft console join GPS tracks in one Tracks section, Insights gains a Connections graph, the planner gains a DPV mission mode, an equipment set can draw itself on an illustrated diver, media gets a map view and drag and drop, and a divelogs.de logbook can be imported. Beneath that are many import and sync fixes, led by Garmin, MacDive, Suunto Cloud and Shearwater CCR corrections. This release upgrades the database (schema 226 to 260) and raises the sync compatibility floor, so please read the upgrade notes at the end.

## ✨ New and improved

### One search row for the dive log

The Dives screen's Search overlay, Filter icon, Advanced Search page and Explore page are replaced by one search row over the live list, opened from the search icon or Cmd/Ctrl+F. It takes plain words, quoted phrases and query syntax such as `depth > 30m`, filters as you type, and lists the five newest matches to jump to. A bare word also matches a dive's type in any app language. The tune button opens a Refine panel holding every filter in eight collapsible groups with a live "Show N dives" button; on desktop it is a side panel with the list still visible, and Insights and Connections use the same panel for their own filter.

Typing a sentence offers an Ask action where the on-device model is available (Apple Intelligence on iOS 26 and macOS 26, ML Kit on Android). Only the sentence reaches the model; every result is computed from your local database. An answer replaces the search query and keeps your other filters, says which words could not be used, and offers Undo. A question about sites, gear, buddies, species, trips or dive centers ("regulators due for service in 30 days") opens that list already filtered. Dives can also be asked about by SAC, how SAC changed through the dive, how steady the final stop was, and by safety findings.

A bookmark button saves the whole search (text plus every Refine axis) as a saved query, synced per diver and managed under Settings > Manage > Saved queries, and an empty field shows saved searches, recents and syntax hints. The same typed query editor and rule builder now reach the Sites, Equipment, Buddies, Dive Centers, Certifications, Courses and Species lists, and every main list shows its count under the title ("34 of 812 dives" while filtered).

### Trips: tabs, cylinders, fills, and a gas record

Every trip now has six tabs (Overview, Itinerary, Gear, Checklist, Dives, Photos). Before departure the Overview is a Prepare page with the countdown and a summary of checklist, gear, itinerary and plan; from the first day it is the story as one scroll with a map per day whose numbered pins match the dive rows. The itinerary works for every trip type with Travel and Rest days, a Day Trip locks to one calendar day, and the service and scrubber alerts fold into one collapsible panel that covers only gear on the trip.

A cylinder board holds the trip's rentals and your own cylinders as slots, each with its mix, pressure, bottle and size, filled one or several at a time with a ledger of every fill. A dive tank can name the slot it was breathed from, so the board follows the dives logged against it, and a Record tab lists every dive tank with the bottle and fill in effect at the time, totals per slot and cost by currency, exportable as CSV. The trip forecasts its fills from the itinerary, today's dives and the fill station's hours ("Tomorrow needs 4, you'll have 1 full. Fill before 5:00 PM."). The Gear tab lists packed gear and cylinder slots together, with a Use set button that packs a whole equipment set.

### A passport for every cylinder

Every cylinder gains a passport page: spec and buoyancy, hydro, VIP and O2 clean status, the current analysed mix with its MOD and END, and a fill history that syncs between devices. The passport id prints as a QR label, singly or in bulk from the equipment list, and can be scanned from the Equipment list's overflow menu or the tank editor. A cylinder you do not own opens read-only with "Use on a dive" and "Add to my gear". On iOS and Android the passport can be written to an NFC tag, which also carries the newest fill so the next diver to tap it sees what is in the tank; nothing on a tag is signed, so a fill read from one is marked "From tag". The trimix blender gains Choose cylinder and Log this fill, fills get their own Submersion CSV, and the tank editor gains "Fill from my cylinders".

### Gear shared and transferred between profiles

Equipment can be shared between the profiles in one library from Settings > Shared data. A profile it is shared with sees it under "Shared with me", can use and maintain it, and can filter by Owner; delete and sharing stay with the owner, and each item page gains a History card of who dived with it. A "Transfer to..." action moves an item with its parts, dive computers and transmitters to another profile. Deleting a profile no longer deletes gear that is shared or on another profile's dives. A new Data Quality check names the same item on two divers' dives at the same time and offers to remove it from either.

### Shared trips and sites belong to whoever made them

Any profile that could see a shared trip or site could delete it for everyone. A shared trip or site is now owned by the profile that created it: only the owner can delete, unshare or merge it, and every other profile gets "Remove from my profile", which hides it for that profile alone. Hidden items come back from Settings > Shared data > Hidden from this profile, and Undo of a site delete or merge now restores hides, species, features, tags and media too.

### Underwater tracks, and one Tracks section

Recordings from a Seacraft ENC console import as underwater tracks through the import wizard, drag and drop or the share sheet. A review page suggests the matching dive, its site and the scooter from your equipment, and an Align action corrects dead-reckoning drift against start and end points and the terrain. Once linked, the dive's 3D seascape draws the measured track instead of the estimated one, with the site's markers and terrain detail. The GPS Log destination becomes Tracks, holding GPS surface tracks and underwater tracks in one list and map with a kind filter, and Dive Edit gains an Underwater Track row under Site. A track is never linked silently; you confirm the suggested dive.

### Connections, and Statistics becomes Insights

Statistics is renamed Insights and gains Connections: an interactive graph of how your buddies, sites, trips, dive centers, equipment, species, tags, dive types, computers and courses relate through shared dives, explored around one entity or as a whole map with presets, with saved maps that sync. Seven detail pages gain "Open in Connections". The Overview card's lifetime average is relabelled "Avg Dives / Year" beside a new "Dives This Year" card, and tapping a country or region on the Geographic page opens those dives.

### A DPV mission in the planner

A plan can be turned into a DPV mission with a "Plan as DPV mission" switch: a route of legs with distance, depth, heading and current, and a team of divers with RMV, swim speed and scooter. Every edit regenerates the plan's segments as the round trip, so the usual profile, deco and gas results apply, and a Mission section names the limiting constraint, each diver's battery reserve and turn pressure, and whether a scooter failure at each waypoint still leaves a way out. Missions sync and travel in plan files.

### An equipment set drawn on a diver

An equipment set can show an illustrated front-and-back diver with every item named on it, turned on with "Show diver figure" on the set edit page. Items can carry a colour (now including Black and White) that tints their artwork, and "Show diver figure on dives" under Settings > Appearance > Dives puts the figure in a dive's equipment card.

### Photos on a map

The media library gains a Map view beside Grid, By dive and Timeline, placing thumbnails on a clustered map at their own GPS fix, their dive's, or their site's. Photos and videos dropped on Media, a dive or a site on desktop now open that screen's importer instead of "Unsupported file type", and the Media section's desktop sidebar becomes app bar tabs. Media stays links only.

### divelogs.de import, PDFs in any language, profile history

A divelogs.de logbook imports from a new card under Transfer > Cloud, with dives, sites, gear, certifications and (on desktop) photos. The logbook export sheet gains a Language picker, every other PDF prints in the app language, and Arabic, Hebrew and Simplified Chinese render correctly. Dive profile edits now keep a revision history in the Profile Editor, and any earlier state can be restored.

### Gas calculators, CCR limits and validated number fields

The MOD calculator gains Rec, OC Tec and CCR Tec modes and a written assessment, a new Gas Density calculator checks a mix or CCR loop at depth against the 5.2 and 6.2 g/L limits, and Settings > Decompression > Oxygen Toxicity gains "ppO2 limits CCR", which now seeds a new CCR plan's setpoints and warns when a diluent would exceed the Dil MOD. The blender's bill can add a priced gas fill line. Across the app, about 190 numeric fields that used to swallow unreadable text (saving 0 m of depth, dropping a tank pressure) now show "Enter a valid number" and refuse to save until fixed.

### Smaller additions

- **Every metric source defaults to Dive Computer** (NDL, deco stops, TTS, gas time remaining, CNS) on a fresh install or new profile; existing libraries keep their preference under Settings > Decompression > Data Sources.
- **The world maps scroll across the date line** and open framed on your dive sites, so Pacific dives are no longer split across the map's edges.
- **Hide built-in tank presets** you never use with a switch on Settings > Tank Presets.
- **Set-aside database copies** from a restore are listed under Backup & Restore with Restore and Delete; nothing prunes them automatically.
- **Discard raw dive computer data** per computer or for all, and Backup settings shows how much space retained backups use. Discarded dives can no longer be re-parsed.
- **The local cache database sweeps itself weekly**; on one install three quarters of it was dead rows.
- **Garmin Connect imports** bring in notes, weight, bottom time, surface interval, water type, CNS, OTU, exit GPS and dives with no FIT file, and Fenix, Epix, Enduro and Tactix watches are named correctly.
- **Dive computers' own tissue state** now survives import, sync and UDDF; nothing shows on screen yet.
- **The Seac Tablet and the Cressi Goa family** gain the Bluetooth transports they need on every platform; neither is yet confirmed on hardware.
- **A first or second stage can carry a Regulator service or O2 clean clock**, added automatically for a new stage.
- **Recent Buddies** sorts by last shared dive, with a new Most Dives card.
- **Settings moved:** 3D Maps joins Offline Maps under Settings > Data > Storage; Equipment condition moves under Settings > Safety; Auto site matching and Tank pressure at surfacing sit under a new Import header; Display options and "Select items" move into the overflow menus.
- **Settings > Units > Visibility scale** explains what it controls and lists each preset's bands.
- **Beta and stable devices now say why they cannot sync**, from both sides.
- **Underwater recordings are called tracks** in all eleven languages.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

**Dive computers and CCR**

- On a Shearwater CCR dive downloaded with a diluent and an oxygen transmitter, the O2 cylinder was labelled with the diluent mix, a bailout without a transmitter imported as Back Gas, and the diluent end pressure kept the post-surfacing bleed-down. Each cylinder now resolves from its own transmitter. Dives already downloaded are repaired with Re-parse all dives on the computer's page.
- On a Shearwater in CCR or SCR mode, a transmitter's role came from the first letter of its name, so a bailout named "OC" imported as the O2 supply and every ppO2 and tissue figure followed. Such a role is now marked unconfirmed, the transmitter registry corrects it, and an import notice explains the rule.
- A CCR dive imported from Subsurface could read "Ongassing" throughout with over 60 minutes of NDL at depth, because a loop dive with no setpoint fell back to open circuit on its first cylinder. Gas direction now follows the inspired gas, a rebreather dive with no loop data withholds tissue figures with a notice, and Subsurface PSCR dives import as semi-closed.
- A dive computer's safety stop was stored as a deco ceiling, so a no-deco dive drew a deco stop band and the flying-after-diving classifier counted it as a deco dive. Already-imported dives are corrected in place.
- With the NDL source set to Dive Computer, samples at a deco stop showed the app's calculated NDL. They now read as in deco: the NDL line ends and the tooltip reads DECO.
- After consolidating two downloads of the same dive, the second computer showed wrong tissue loading, ceiling and CNS (one Garmin read CNS 593% instead of 31%) because it borrowed the primary's tanks and gas switches. Each computer is now analysed on its own data.

The resume point now moves only for computers that deliver oldest-first, so the missed dives are offered again next time. The Symbios transfer stall itself is still being diagnosed.
- A Seac Tablet still failed on Android after the first fix because another service's write and notify pair was chosen over the Seac service; the Seac service now wins. Still unconfirmed on hardware.
- A dive whose computer could not report a date was dated to the year -1 and sorted to the start of the logbook, dragging numbering and tissue carry-over with it. A fresh download now dates it to the download time, where it can be corrected.

## ⚠️ Upgrade notes

- **The database upgrades from schema 226 to 260.** The migration runs once on first launch and the app takes an automatic backup beforehand, so there is nothing you need to do first. The first background sweep afterwards rebuilds cached sensor summaries once for dives with a profile, and safety reviews on rebreather dives and dives with deco stops recompute on next view, keeping each finding's dismissal.
- **Update every device in your sync library.** This release raises the sync compatibility floor from 224 to 240, so a device still on v1.8.0 or earlier stops receiving changes from an updated device until it updates too. Its own changes still arrive on the updated device, so nothing is lost, but the older device falls behind until you update it.
- **Statistics is now Insights**, GPS Log is now Tracks, and the Explore page, Advanced Search and the old Filter sheet are retired in favour of the search row and the Refine panel. Old links redirect, a customised bottom bar or rail keeps its slot, Cmd/Ctrl+F opens the search row (it no longer opens Advanced Search), and Cmd/Ctrl+E is gone. The Underwater Routes tile under Settings > Manage and the GPS Logger quick action are replaced by Tracks.
- **Quoted text in a search is now an exact phrase**, so a saved query with quoted multi-word text becomes stricter. A hyphen inside a word is part of the word (Abu-Nuhas, U-352); only a leading hyphen negates a term.
- **A shared trip or site can no longer be deleted, unshared or merged by a profile that did not create it**; those profiles get "Remove from my profile" instead, and a bulk delete hides the items it does not own.
- **Every metric source defaults to Dive Computer only on a fresh install or new profile.** An existing library keeps its stored preference; change it per metric under Settings > Decompression > Data Sources.
- **Limit depths (MOD, MND) are rounded down everywhere**, so EAN32 at 1.4 reads 33 m instead of 34 m. The "ppO2 limits" setting is renamed "ppO2 limits OC", and the new CCR limits default to 0.7 / 1.3 / 1.6.
- **Settings moved:** 3D Maps is inside Settings > Data > Storage > Offline Maps; Equipment condition is under Settings > Safety; Auto site matching and Tank pressure at surfacing are under Settings > Data > Import; Display options on Dive and Site Details and "Select items" on every list are in the overflow menu.
- **The trip page's header cards, pinned story band and trip-level map are gone**, replaced by the tabs. The scrubber margin and fill forecast count dive days from the itinerary's per-day plan, so a rebreather diver's expected scrubber use can change on a trip with such days. Choosing Day Trip locks the end date to the start date. An itinerary day with no planned count no longer borrows a guess from past trips for the fill forecast, and a day already stored above 12 planned dives is left as it is but cannot be saved again until it is brought within the cap. The "needs service before this trip" warning now covers only gear packed for the trip or on its cylinder board, so a trip with nothing packed shows none.
- **The equipment list's default view is now "Current Equipment"**; "All Equipment" really shows retired and sold gear.
- **Only new imports benefit from the importer fixes** (MacDive times, seconds, gas switches and per-cylinder duration; Suunto Cloud times; Subsurface current and second computers; Garmin exit GPS and Connect fields; two-digit years; trip placement; DiveLogDT duplicate computers). Use Re-parse all dives for a Shearwater CCR download, Replace Source or "Resync from original file" where offered, and otherwise re-import the file and consolidate duplicates. Dives already imported from Garmin Connect must be deleted and imported again. Apple Watch dives imported before this release keep their shifted times.
- **Data Quality findings are not repaired automatically.** The inbox shows a "new checks available" banner; run Rescan or the library scan to replace the old pressure, consumption and unknown-transmitter findings.
- **A plan file that contains a DPV mission is written in plan file format 3**, which older builds cannot open; plans without a mission are unchanged.
- **Exported file names change:** letters of any script are kept, a plan name's hyphen becomes an underscore, and names are capped at 100 bytes.
- **New permissions:** camera access is requested on iOS, Android and macOS the first time you scan a cylinder label, and Android gains the NFC permission. On Android, Submersion now also offers itself for shared FIT and ZIP files.
- **Set-aside database copies and discarded raw data:** nothing prunes set-aside copies, so delete them from Backup & Restore when no longer needed, and a dive whose raw data you discard can no longer be re-parsed after a libdivecomputer fix.
- **Diver settings reset by an earlier mixed-build sync are not restored**; set them again once every device is on this release.
- **No minimum operating system change** on macOS, Windows, iOS, Android or Linux.
