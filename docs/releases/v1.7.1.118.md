# 🎉 Submersion v1.7.1.118 Release Notes

v1.7.1 is a follow-up to v1.7.0, built almost entirely from what users reported in the days after that launch. The gas calculators are rebuilt and finally correct in imperial units, encrypted cloud sync works on Android again, photo attachment works on Windows and Linux, and weather and oxygen-exposure text now appear in your language and your units. Everything upgrades automatically the first time you open the app.

---

## ✨ New and improved

### The gas calculators are rebuilt, and correct in imperial

If you dive in imperial units, Rock Bottom and Consumption were giving answers that made no sense: a reserve for a 100 ft dive came back as 18 cu ft. The SAC sliders never converted at all, and their imperial range was so far off-scale that no realistic breathing rate could be selected anywhere on the slider. All four calculators now run on unit-safe inputs, with the math covered by independently computed test vectors. Rock Bottom also adds a problem-solving time at depth (one minute by default), which is standard minimum-gas practice, and uses the ascent rate you actually set. Consumption uses each cylinder's own working pressure instead of assuming a 200 bar fill, so an HP100 is no longer judged against a steel 12's numbers.

### Best Mix no longer recommends a gas you have already exceeded

Asking for the best mix at 111 ft with a 1.4 ppO₂ limit used to return EAN32, whose own maximum operating depth is 110.7 ft, shallower than the dive you asked about. Oxygen now rounds down, so that request returns 31% with 4.4 ft of margin, and every candidate is shown with its own MOD, END, and gas density. When the narcosis limit forces helium into the answer, the helium-free mix is shown alongside it with its own limits flagged, so you see the trade-off rather than being quietly handed trimix.

### Weather and oxygen exposure in your language and your units

Weather descriptions were generated as English prose when they were fetched and then frozen into your logbook, so a German or Japanese logbook still read "Partly cloudy, light breeze." Submersion now stores the weather code and renders the description at display time, in your language. Surface pressure gains inHg, swell height converts, and the CNS and oxygen card is translated. Only weather that Submersion fetched itself is refreshed; descriptions you typed or imported are left exactly as they are.

### Smaller additions

- **A DD.MM.YYYY date format**, standard in Germany and much of Central Europe, is now in the date-format picker.
- **Certification names fill themselves in** as "PADI : Open Water" once you pick an agency and level, and stop updating as soon as you type your own title.
- **Subsurface and UDDF imports keep your computer's stop schedule.** Both importers were discarding the stop depths, so the Computer ceiling and deco-stop band had nothing to draw for those dives.
- **The ceiling line always shows the calculated ceiling.** Its Computer setting drew the same stepped data as the deco-stop band beside it, so the source selector (and its now-inert setting) is gone. The Computer/Calculated toggle stays on the deco stops, where the two genuinely differ.

## 🐞 Bug fixes

- **Encrypted cloud sync and backups work on Android.** They were broken for every Android user: the passphrase appeared to fail and every sync died on a native crypto error. Existing encrypted libraries and backups keep working, with nothing to re-do.
- **Devices no longer re-upload your whole library periodically.** A device could wrongly read its own files in the cloud as another device's, adopt a new identity, and upload a fresh full copy, repeatedly. (Orphaned `ssv1.<old-id>.*` files already left in your storage can be deleted by hand; otherwise they clear on their own after a year.)
- **The same dive computer no longer appears twice across Apple devices.** Bluetooth identifiers are host-specific and were being treated as the computer's identity; computers are now matched by manufacturer, model, and serial. Existing duplicates are not merged automatically.
- **Photos attach correctly on Windows and Linux.** Imports reported success and then showed "File not found," and photos plainly inside the dive window were rejected as unmatched. Desktop imports now store the real file path and read capture time from the photo's own EXIF data rather than the file's copy date. The Files tab can also assign a photo the date matcher rejected, and the Done button no longer looks stuck when you are staging files. **Note:** photos imported on Windows before this fix cannot be recovered and need to be removed and added again.
- **Setting up a new device from a Dropbox backup** no longer fails with errors while writing your diver settings.
- **You can sign a dive on iOS.** Trying to draw dragged the signature sheet around instead of leaving a mark.
- **Dashboard and Planning buttons go where they say.** "Add gear," the gear service chips, "Flying after diving," and an imported dive center's View action all crashed to Page Not Found. The insurance chip, previously the only dashboard chip that did nothing, now opens the insurance page.
- **Navigation from a dive to its site and back** works again, including after editing the site.
- **Large dive numbers fit their badge** instead of wrapping and overflowing the circle at four or five digits.
- **Dive centers show their state or province**, which was being dropped from every location display.

## 🔧 Under the hood

One small database change stores the raw weather code so descriptions can be rendered in your language; it applies automatically on first launch. The gas calculator math moved into a pure, testable layer behind 106 new tests, including independently computed acceptance vectors, which is the direct reason it shipped broken: none of it was testable before. Fifty new pieces of text are translated across all ten non-English languages.
