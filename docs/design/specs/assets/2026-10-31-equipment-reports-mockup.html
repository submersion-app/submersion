Proof of care, item by item. Useful when selling gear or for a warranty claim. The maintenance log export has the same data as a spreadsheet.

Page one is always the due list. History pages follow when turned on. Picking a trip lists only the gear packed for it, and date-based dues are judged at the end of that trip.
