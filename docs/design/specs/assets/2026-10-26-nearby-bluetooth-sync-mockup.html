Nearby devices: where it lives and what you see

Three screens and one chip. First, pick the entry point; then react to the rest.

1. Entry point: which of these is "next to the other backends"?

A

A row in Settings > Data, right under Sync

"Nearby devices" sits in the Your data list with its own status line ("2 devices, synced 5 min ago"). The Sync page stays about the cloud account.

B

A section inside the Sync page

The Sync page gets a "Nearby devices" block under the account row: the switch and the paired list live there, one page for everything sync.
