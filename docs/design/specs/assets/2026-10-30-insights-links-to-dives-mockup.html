What happens when you tap a bar or a pie slice?

Today a touch on a bar shows its number, and that is the only way to read it on a phone. A tap can't do both jobs unless one of them moves. Legend rows and horizontal bar rows have nothing hidden behind a touch, so they open straight away in both.

A

Show the number first, then open

The first tap marks the bar and shows a strip with its number. Tapping the strip opens the dives. Nothing jumps away from you when you only wanted to read a value.

B

Open straight away

One tap and you are in the dive list, as with countries and calendar days. To read a bar's number on a phone you press and hold, which few people would discover.
