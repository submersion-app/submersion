What changed from today

  • Tank column: the size (AL80) leads. A tank you named shows its name, with size and role underneath. The role is hidden on a plain single back-gas dive, as today.
  • Units sit once in the column header, so the numbers are bare and line up.
  • One quiet style for everything under a row, always "label value" joined by dots. The bold blue, the pink and the brackets are gone.
  • Litres used moves into that line as "Gas used".
  • The ppO₂ behind MOD is the same for every tank, so it is said once under the table instead of on every row.
  • Gear check-in becomes a small icon at the end of the tank's detail line.
  • Kept in red: the "role read from the transmitter's name" warning, since it is a warning.
