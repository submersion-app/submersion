# How-To Guides

These guides are for divers who are new to Submersion. Each one takes a single goal, such as getting sync working or bringing your dives over from another app, and walks it from start to finish, one step and one screen at a time.

The rest of this user guide is a reference that explains every option of each feature. The how-to guides link into it wherever you want more detail, so you can follow a guide without reading the reference first.

## Get set up

- [Set Up Sync, Backups and Photos](how-to-sync-backups-photos.md): your logbook on your phone and computer, backed up, with photos on both.
- [Set Up a Shared Site Library](how-to-shared-site-library.md): one list of dive sites for your club or dive group.

## Use the app

- [Log GPS Tracks on Your Phone](how-to-gps-track-logging.md): record a boat day so each dive gets its position.
- [Log a Dive from Your Computer](how-to-log-a-dive.md): download your dives and turn one into a complete log entry.
- [Customize the Display](how-to-customize-display.md): your theme, units, Dashboard, dive list and navigation.

## Moving from another app

Each of these guides shows what comes across from your old app and what does not, before you start, so you know what to expect.

- [Moving from MacDive to Submersion](how-to-move-from-macdive.md): your whole MacDive logbook, read straight from its database.
- [Moving from Subsurface to Submersion](how-to-move-from-subsurface.md): dives, trips, sites and photos from your Subsurface log.
- [Moving from Shearwater Cloud to Submersion](how-to-move-from-shearwater-cloud.md): dives and sites from the Shearwater Cloud desktop app.
- [Moving from Diving Log to Submersion](how-to-move-from-diving-log.md): your Diving Log database, gear and trips included.
- [Moving from Garmin Connect to Submersion](how-to-move-from-garmin-connect.md): dives from your Garmin watch, straight from your account.
- [Moving from Suunto to Submersion](how-to-move-from-suunto.md): dives and your journal from the Suunto app.
- [Moving from divelogs.de to Submersion](how-to-move-from-divelogs-de.md): your logbook, sites, gear and photos from divelogs.de.
- [Moving from DAN DiveCloud to Submersion](how-to-move-from-dan-divecloud.md): dives and photos exported from DAN DiveCloud.
