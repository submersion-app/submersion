# Certifications and Courses

Keep your diving qualifications and training history in one place: every
certification card you hold, with photos and expiry and refresher reminders,
and every course you have taken, with its requirements, training dives and
instructor signatures.

> [!NOTE]
> **Where to find it:** **Certifications** and **Courses** in the navigation.
> When a certification needs attention, the Dashboard's
> **Certification currency** chip opens it too.

<!-- screenshot: images/certifications-and-courses/cert-wallet.png: certification wallet showing agency-branded cards -->

## Certifications

### Adding a certification

Tap **Add Certification** on the Certifications list.

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Agency** | Required. PADI, SSI, NAUI, SDI, TDI, GUE, RAID, BSAC, CMAS, IANTD, PSAI, FFESSM, ACUC, DAN or Other, plus any agencies you add yourself |
| **Certification** | Required. Pick from the agency's catalog, grouped into **Progression** and **Specialties**, or choose **Add custom certification...** |
| **Add another recognition** | For a card that one certification also counts as another, for example a crossover. These show as "Also recognized as" |
| **Name on card** | Optional, when the card's wording differs from the catalog name |
| **Card Number** | Your diver number from the card |
| **Issue Date** / **Expiry Date** | Leave the expiry empty for certifications that do not expire |
| **Instructor** | Pick a buddy, or type the **Instructor Name** and **Instructor Number** |
| **Card Photos** | Front and back of the physical card: **Take Photo** or **Choose from Gallery** |
| **Notes** | Anything else |

To add your own agencies, or certifications missing from an agency's catalog,
go to **Settings > Manage > Certification Agencies**. Built-in certifications
cannot be changed, but you can add your own to any agency.

### Expiry

A certification with an expiry date warns you as the date approaches. Within 90
days of expiry its detail page shows "Expires in N days" and its wallet card is
marked **EXPIRING**; after the date the card reads **EXPIRED** and the detail
page says "This certification has expired". Certifications without an expiry
date show **No Expiration**.

### Currency: refreshers and renewals

Some qualifications lapse without a printed expiry date: a recreational card
after a year without diving, a professional membership every year, a first aid
course every two years. Submersion tracks these as **currency** on each
certification's detail page, and warns you before a certification lapses.

These rules are built in:

| Rule | Applies to | Lapses after | Counts from |
|------|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| PADI refresher (ReActivate) | PADI recreational and professional levels | 1 year | Your last dive |
| SSI Scuba Skills Update | SSI recreational and professional levels | 1 year | Your last dive |
| Refresher | Recreational, technical and professional levels of the other agencies | 1 year | Your last dive |
| First aid and CPR renewal | First aid, oxygen provider and DAN courses | 2 years | Your last renewal |
| Professional membership renewal | Dive guide, divemaster and instructor levels | 1 year | Your last renewal |
| GUE revalidation | GUE certifications | 3 years | Your last renewal |
| FFESSM licence and medical certificate | FFESSM certifications | 1 year | Your last renewal |
| Cave currency | Cavern and cave certifications | 1 year | Your last cave or cavern dive |
| Rebreather currency | Rebreather certifications | 6 months | Your last CCR or SCR dive |
| Decompression currency | Decompression, trimix and technical certifications | 1 year | Your last technical dive |

A rule that counts from a renewal uses the card's expiry date or the last
renewal you logged, whichever is later, and otherwise the issue date. On a
certification's **Currency** section you can:

- **Log refresher** when you take a refresher, renewal or revalidation.
- **Edit interval** to change when it lapses and how early it warns.
- Choose **Which dives count** by dive type or dive mode.
- **Mute** a rule that does not apply to you.

Change the rules themselves, or add your own, in
**Settings > Manage > Certification currency**. Editing a built-in rule saves your own copy in its
place.

### The certification wallet

Tap **Wallet View** on the Certifications list to see every certification as
an agency-branded card. Tap a card to flip it: the back shows your card photo,
or a generated back with the instructor's details. Below each card, the share
button shares it and **More options** opens **Share**, **View Details** and
**Edit** (a long press opens the same menu).

### Sharing a card

**Share Certification** offers two formats:

- **Share as Card:** the agency-branded card as an image.
- **Share as Certificate:** a formal certificate with your name, the
  certification, agency, card number and issue date.

## Courses

A course records your training: when it started and finished, who taught it,
what it requires, and the training dives you made.

<!-- screenshot: images/certifications-and-courses/course-detail.png: course detail showing requirements, training dives and earned certification -->

### Adding a course

Tap **Add Course** on the Courses list.

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Course Name** | Required, for example "Open Water Diver" |
| **Agency** | The same agencies as certifications |
| **Start Date** | The first day of the course |
| **Completed** / **Completion Date** | Switch on and set the date when you finish |
| **Instructor** | Pick a buddy, or type the **Instructor Name** and **Instructor Number** |
| **Location** | The dive center or shop |
| **Notes** | Anything else |
| **Earned Certification** | The certification you received for this course |

The instructor picker lists buddies holding an instructor-level certification
first. Picking one fills in their name and, for an instructor, their instructor
number.

### Requirements

The **Requirements** section tracks what the course asks of you, and counts
"N of M complete". **Add requirement** creates either a **Dive requirement**
(a number of **Required dives**, met by linking dives) or a **Check-off item**
(a skill or prerequisite you tick off). **Add from template** adds a standard
set of requirements for courses such as Advanced Open Water, Rescue Diver,
Deep, Night, Wreck or Enriched Air Nitrox. A dive requirement suggests dives
from your log that could count toward it.

### Training dives

In the dive form, pick the course in the **Course** field to make the dive one
of its training dives. The course's **Training Dives** section lists them all.

### Instructor signatures

A dive that belongs to a course shows a **Training Signature** section on its
detail page. Tap **Capture Instructor Signature** to have your instructor sign
on the screen; the signature is kept with that dive. Buddies can sign your
dives too; see [Buddies & Dive Centers](buddies-and-dive-centers.md).

### Finishing a course

**Mark as Completed** on the course page sets today as the completion date.
Link the certification you earned under **Earned Certification**, and the two
records link to each other. **Export Training Log** creates a PDF of the course
and its training dives.

The Courses list can show **All**, **In Progress** or **Completed** courses.
