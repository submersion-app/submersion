# Certifications and Courses

Keep your diving qualifications and training history in one place — track every certification card you hold and every course you have taken, with photos, expiry reminders, and an instructor-signed training log.

> [!NOTE]
> **Where to find it:** Bottom navigation or sidebar &rarr; **Certifications** for your certification wallet; **Courses** for your training records. Both sections are also reachable from the **Dashboard**.

<!-- screenshot: images/certifications-and-courses/cert-wallet.png — certification wallet showing agency-branded cards in stack view -->

## Certifications

### Adding a certification

Tap the **+** button on the Certifications list to open the add form. The following fields are available:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Name** | Yes | Free text — e.g. "Open Water Diver" |
| **Agency** | Yes | Pick from: PADI, SSI, NAUI, SDI, TDI, GUE, RAID, BSAC, CMAS, IANTD, PSAI, Other |
| **Level** | No | Pick from a standard list (Open Water, Advanced Open Water, Rescue Diver, Divemaster, Instructor, Master Instructor, Course Director, Nitrox, Advanced Nitrox, Decompression, Trimix, Cavern, Cave, Wreck, Sidemount, Rebreather, Tech Diver, Other) |
| **Card number** | No | Your diver identification number from the card |
| **Issue date** | No | The date the certification was issued |
| **Expiry date** | No | Leave blank for certifications that do not expire |
| **Instructor name** | No | Name of the instructor who certified you |
| **Instructor number** | No | Instructor's certification number |
| **Notes** | No | Any additional information |

Tap **Save** (or **Add** on the button) to create the record.

### Card photos

Each certification stores a **front photo** and a **back photo** of the physical card. Tap either photo slot to take a new picture with the camera or choose one from your photo library.

Photos let you carry a visual copy of your card without the physical card, and they appear on the back face when you flip a card in the wallet view.

### Expiry warnings

Submersion tracks expiry automatically using the date you entered:

- **Expires in X days** — shown when fewer than 30 days remain.
- **Expires in X months** — shown when between 30 and 90 days remain.
- **EXPIRING badge** — displayed on the card in the wallet when fewer than 90 days remain.
- **EXPIRED badge** — displayed when the expiry date has passed.
- The Dashboard wallet widget shows a warning count badge if any certification is expired or expiring within 90 days.

Certifications with no expiry date show "No expiry."

### The certification wallet

The wallet view displays every certification as an agency-branded card at credit-card proportions. Each card uses the colours associated with the issuing agency (for example, PADI cards use a blue gradient, NAUI cards use green). Swipe left or right to move between cards. Tap a card to flip it and reveal the back, which shows your card photo if you uploaded one, or a generated back face with instructor details.

Long-press a card to open a context menu with options to **Share**, **View Details**, or **Edit** the certification.

> [!TIP]
> Tap the wallet icon in the top-right corner of the Certifications list to jump straight to the wallet view.

### Sharing a card

From the wallet view, tap the share button (bottom-right) or use the context menu to open the share sheet. Two formats are available:

- **Share as Card** — renders the agency-branded card exactly as it looks on screen and shares it as a PNG image.
- **Share as Certificate** — generates a formal white-background certificate with your name, the certification name, agency, card number, and issue date.

Both formats can be sent to any app on your device that accepts images.

## Courses

Courses let you record a training course as a distinct entity — with start and completion dates, instructor information, and a list of the training dives you completed during the course.

<!-- screenshot: images/certifications-and-courses/course-detail.png — course detail showing linked training dives and earned certification -->

### Adding a course

Tap **+** on the Courses list. The following fields are available:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Name** | Yes | Course title — e.g. "Advanced Open Water" |
| **Agency** | Yes | Same agency list as certifications |
| **Start date** | Yes | First day of the course |
| **Completion date** | No | Toggle "Completed" on to set this; leave off for courses still in progress |
| **Instructor** | No | Pick from your Buddies list (certified instructors only) or type a name and number manually |
| **Instructor number** | No | Instructor's certification number |
| **Location** | No | Dive centre or shop where the course took place |
| **Notes** | No | Any additional information |
| **Earned certification** | No | Link to the certification record you received on completing the course |

> [!TIP]
> If the instructor is already in your Buddies list with a certification level recorded, they appear in the instructor dropdown and their name fills in automatically. You can still type a name manually if the instructor is not in your contacts.

### Linking dives to a course

Open any dive in the **Dive Log** and edit it. The edit form includes a **Course** picker showing your in-progress courses. Select a course to associate that dive with it. The association appears on the dive detail page and the dive is listed in the course's training dives section.

A course's detail page shows all linked training dives, so you can review every dive that was part of the course in one place.

### Instructor signatures

When a dive is part of a course, the dive detail page shows a **Training Signature** section. Tap **Add Signature** to open a signature capture sheet where the instructor can draw their signature and confirm their name. The signature is saved against that individual dive.

To view a saved signature, open the dive detail and scroll to the signatures section.

> [!TIP]
> Signatures are captured per dive, which matches the standard training log format used by most agencies. A signed dive is a record that the instructor was present and reviewed that specific dive.

### The earned certification link

When you complete a course and earn a certification, you can link the two records so navigation between them is easy. On the course edit form, use the **Earned Certification** picker to select the certification. On the certification list or detail page, you can navigate directly to the course that produced it. The link is bidirectional.

### Course status

A course is either **In Progress** (no completion date set) or **Completed** (completion date set). The Courses list can be filtered by status so you can quickly see what you are currently working on.
