# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen: a dense, at-a-glance view of your diving, the things that need attention, and quick ways into the rest of the app. It is made of panels laid out in rows that always run edge to edge. You pick a ready-made layout and can then show, hide and reorder the panels.

> [!NOTE]
> **Where to find it:** Tap **Home**, the first item on the phone's bottom bar or the top of the navigation rail. It is the first screen you see when you open Submersion.

<!-- screenshot: images/dashboard/overview.png: populated dashboard -->

## Header

A slim bar across the top holds:

- a **+** button for the quick actions (see below),
- the **status lights** (see below),
- when you have more than one [diver profile](diver-profile.md), your avatar. Tap it to switch diver,
- and the Submersion logo at the end of the bar. Tap it for **About Submersion**: the version you are running and when it was built.

Tap **+** for the **quick actions**:

- **Log Dive** (log manually or download from a computer),
- **Plan Dive** (the [dive planner](planning.md)),
- **Swap with a buddy** (not in a web browser),
- and the **Emergency card** (hotlines, chambers and your medical details; see [Safety](safety.md)).

On a phone the status lights sit in a strip under the bar.

## Status lights

The status lights show what needs your attention. Each has a colour and a short label: green when all is well, amber when something is due soon, and red when it needs action. Tap a light to open the screen behind it; a gear light, for example, opens that piece of equipment. Red and amber lights come first.

| Light | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| **Dive currency** | Days since your last dive, or "Dove today". |
| **No-fly timer** | Time left before flying is advised after your last dive. |
| **Flight dive window** | Before a planned flight, how long you can still dive. |
| **Gear service** | The equipment whose service is due soonest, or overdue. |
| **Insurance** | Whether your dive insurance is current, expiring, or missing. |
| **Certification currency** | Certifications that need refreshing. |
| **Documents** | Documents that are expiring or have expired. |
| **Upcoming trip** | A countdown to your next [trip](trips.md). |
| **Active checklist** | A pre-dive checklist you have started. |
| **Course progress** | How far through a [course](certifications-and-courses.md) you are. |
| **Media uploads** | Photos and videos still waiting to upload. |
| **Backup age** | When you last backed up, or "No backup yet". |
| **Sync status** | Whether every change has synced. |
| **Nearby device** | A paired device that cannot sync. |
| **Data quality** | How many [data issues](data-quality-assistant.md) are waiting. |

On a phone the lights sit in a strip under the bar: any red or amber light is named, and the rest are folded into one **all clear** count. Tap the count to see every light.

You can turn any light off (see [Customizing the Dashboard](#customizing-the-dashboard)), but a few safety warnings show anyway: overdue gear, an expired insurance policy, a closed flight window, and a certification that lapsed on a date you entered (a card expiry or a logged renewal).

## Panels

Tap any panel to open the full screen behind it.

| Panel | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| **Key numbers** | A row of headline numbers, each with a comparison and, where there is one, a small trend line: dives, time underwater, days since your last dive, average gas consumption, species seen (with how many are new this year) and days to your next trip. Deepest dive this year and average depth can be added. |
| **Activity calendar** | One square per day for the last twelve months (six on a phone), darker on days with more dives. |
| **Gear service** | The equipment nearest its service, each with a bar showing how far through its interval it is. Overdue gear comes first. |
| **Coming due** | The next gear services, insurance renewal, certifications and documents, soonest first. |
| **Next trip** | Your next trip's dates, countdown and checklist progress. |
| **Latest profile** | The depth profile of your most recent dive. |
| **Latest dive vs average** | Your most recent dive's depth, runtime and gas consumption against your averages over the last twelve months. |
| **Recent dives** | Your latest dives as a table. Tap a row to open the dive. |
| **SAC trend** or **RMV trend** | Your gas consumption over your last 30 dives, as SAC (bar a minute) or RMV (litres a minute), depending on which you read in [settings](settings.md). |
| **Depth mix** | How many of your dives reached each depth band. |
| **Dives per month** | Your dives in each of the last twelve months. |
| **Gas mix**, **Dive types** | The share of your dives on air, nitrox and trimix, and by dive type. |
| **Top sites**, **Top buddies** | The sites and buddies you dive with most. |
| **Species seen lately** | The marine life you have seen most on your recent dives. |
| **Recent media** | Your latest photos and videos. |
| **Recent sites** | A map of the sites you dived most recently. |
| **On this day** | A dive you made on today's date in an earlier year. |
| **Checklists**, **Courses in progress**, **Waiting for download** | Your pre-dive and trip checklists, courses under way, and logged dives waiting for your dive computer. |

**Next trip**, **Species seen lately**, **Recent media**, **Recent sites**, **On this day**, **Checklists**, **Courses in progress** and **Waiting for download** appear only when they have something to show, so a layout never has empty boxes.

Before you have logged any dives, a panel under the key numbers offers to **Log Dive** or **Import** your dives: the quickest way to get started.

## Layouts

There are four ready-made layouts:

| Layout | Leads with |
|--------|------------|
| **Balanced** (the default) | Key numbers, your activity and gear, your latest dive, then charts and memories. |
| **Ready** | What is coming up: gear service, things coming due, your next trip. |
| **Trends** | How your diving is going: activity, gas consumption, depth and rankings. |
| **Logbook** | Your latest dive in detail, then recent dives, photos and species. |

## Customizing the Dashboard

Open **Settings > Appearance > Home**:

- **Layout**: pick one of the four layouts. If you have changed the panels yourself, you are asked before your arrangement is replaced.
- **Panels**: show or hide each panel, and drag to reorder them. Changing them makes the layout your own. Tap **Key numbers** to choose which numbers it shows. **Reset to default** returns to the Balanced layout.
- **Status chips**: turn each status light on or off.

These choices are kept on each device, so your phone and your computer can use different layouts. If you hide every panel, the Dashboard says so and offers **Customize Home** to bring them back.

> [!TIP]
> Keep your equipment service dates and insurance expiry up to date in [Equipment](equipment.md) and [Diver Profile](diver-profile.md), so the lights can warn you before a deadline passes.
