# Photos for the species identification ONNX check

Two underwater photos the ONNX engine check
(`integration_test/species_id_onnx/species_id_onnx_test.dart`) runs the
species pack's models on. Both are works of NOAA Fisheries staff, taken as
part of their official duties, so they are in the public domain in the United
States as works of the US federal government (Wikimedia Commons licence
template `PD-USGov-NOAA`).

Each was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons as a PNG, scaled to 1024 pixels
wide and saved as a JPEG with no metadata (no EXIF orientation, no colour
profile). Nothing else was changed.

| File | Shows | Author | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `pink_anemonefish.jpg` (1024 x 768) | A pink anemonefish (*Amphiprion perideraion*) in a sea anemone off Pagan, Northern Mariana Islands, 2022 | NOAA Fisheries/Raymond Boland | [Commons: Pink anemone fish Pagan 2022.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pink_anemone_fish_Pagan_2022.png), from NOAA's story map [NOAA divers survey reefs and habitats in the Mariana Islands](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/21dae1db38774f189dd23510e1f82b0a) | Public domain (US federal government work, NOAA) |
| `goatfish_school.jpg` (1024 x 666) | A school of mimic goatfish (*Mulloidichthys mimicus*) and yellowfin goatfish (*M. vanicolensis*) over a coral reef off Howland Island, 2023 | NOAA Fisheries/Mia Lamirand | [Commons: Mimic goatfish coral reef Howland Island 2023.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mimic_goatfish_coral_reef_Howland_Island_2023.png), from NOAA's story map [Supporting below surface science](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/c0ba9a4cb8d54e3281d7e91f45c6777a) | Public domain (US federal government work, NOAA) |

Both licences and credits were checked on 2026-11-22 on the Commons file
pages (template `PD-NOAA`, which redirects to `PD-USGov-NOAA`) and in the
captions of NOAA's own story maps, which credit each photo to the NOAA
Fisheries photographer named above.
